and every attempted address agree with the ideal code when every REACHED read

diagnostics, NOT an installed accepted two-transaction attack or a full independence
proof for Rc10. No conclusion about actual Solana syscall totality is inferred.

have to establish account acceptance in the incompatible worlds.
