## Why no NDT closure is claimed

The existing native windows and extraction equalities do not yet constitute the full native/model/physical installation for four_dots_one. In particular, choosing a permitted opaque reply FROM the model output proves representability, not that the concrete native call returned that model output in the same execution. Closing it needs the installed callee bridge and source-order/loop/frame transport on the concrete native run. The wrapper inherits this obligation for each actual child. The incoming query_values edge additionally needs the original proof/opening and SAME g canonicality/frame certificate through the continuation. NFC's832-byte window and pure offset/frame laws do not provide that whole certificate. NFB's imported committed snapshot still has no final ledger/validator or closed root certificate. These missing joins are not turned into cryptographic assumptions or arbitrary new root premises.
