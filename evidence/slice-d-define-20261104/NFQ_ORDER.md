# Bottom-up D order

**Plan, not closure.** NFQ_ORDER.json enumerates every OPEN function, exact callee rows and existing receipt packets, inventory domains, SCC and certificate shape. Components are ordered callee before caller. Conservative receiver ambiguity can create artificial cycles; a cycle must be resolved against frozen receiver types before claiming a real recursive certificate.

## First five candidates with existing interface shapes

1. `crates/aspis-r1-cost-probe/src/compact.rs::compact_row`: all u8, pure Bool; NCO extracted reference/cohort law and scalar body contract exist.
2. `crates/aspis-r1-cost-probe/src/r1_wire.rs::row_shape`: all u8, Option<(u8, usize)>; NCO extracted reference/cohort law and controls exist; no substitution of an unadmitted returned-reference carrier.
3. `crates/aspis-r1-cost-probe/src/transport.rs::Variant::folds`: all three variants; scalar result, NBG_Transport emitted/cohort equations. Actual-body contract may execute scalar callees directly; no stub_verified interface needed.
4. `crates/aspis-r1-cost-probe/src/transport.rs::Variant::final_len`: all three variants; scalar result, NBG_Transport emitted/cohort equations. Actual-body contract may execute scalar callees directly; no stub_verified interface needed.
5. `crates/aspis-r1-cost-probe/src/verifier.rs::checkpoint_size`: all three variants; scalar result, NBG_Transport emitted/cohort equations. Actual-body contract may execute scalar callees directly; no stub_verified interface needed.

These are candidates for certification now, not an assertion that their inherited packets pass ax2 or current rule16 review. Prior NCO/transport native inputs must be revalidated before credit. No new extraction is needed if those exact extracted references and pins are reused. Order folds before final_len before checkpoint_size.

## TX1 spine priority after scalar leaves

prepare -> Prepared::new / powers / verify_observed requires full Result, owned prepared/scratch carriers, original proof/public bytes and digest order. Compact expansion/checking and semantic transcript depend on compact_row/row_shape; checkpoint writer depends on checkpoint_size and the existing checkpoint opaque packet. query_loop remains an opaque OPEN Q boundary pending NFP. Do not hold independent D leaves behind it.

finish_query_dot has Q-boundary EbyKDot::push (OPEN in NFK, later Q support must be validated); it may proceed once that callee packet is imported with domain proof. Pool settlement consumer is outside the inventory and owned by NFO; no new outside-slice bridge is attempted.
