`NFF_LoggedRaw` copies the actual emitted wrapping expression and replaces ONLY its sixteen operand products, in source order. `NFF_LoggedPush` and `NFF_LoggedTail` preserve both rejection paths and all actual emitted continuations. These equalities do not assume native whole-function output equality. They also do not, on their own, establish physical execution correspondence.

No `stub_verified` or `proof_for_contract` is used by these new native windows. No new contract interface shape is admitted. The Option<fn> quarantine remains; none of the new properties depends on a function-pointer value. No mark call is reached in the incoming cuts; no observer behavior is credited.
