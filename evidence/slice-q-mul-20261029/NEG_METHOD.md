Evaluate actual frozen extraction first, then admissible product abstraction and native loop-step association. Exact-product assumptions must be proved admissible in Lean; replacing a source body alone does not prove native association.

## First extraction and abstraction evidence

NEG_ProductAbstraction proves that an exact modular-product relation contains the real mathematical machine product, pointwise logs preserve this fact, and universal properties transfer. The relation is a singleton, so placing `assume(p == a*b)` in Kani still introduces multiplication and is not a solver solution. A free product log avoids multiplication, but its per-site association to native operands and outputs must be separately proved. The generic Lean theorem alone does not establish that association.
