# Stub ledger

No new `kani::stub` is used in this job. No field multiplication is submitted to Kani. No field, hash, runtime syscall or whole protocol loop is modeled by a new arbitrary successful stub. Native parent cuts take explicit arbitrary child Results/callbacks; they are checked as caller-control fragments, not marked whole-native passes. Their source-model associations use the named lower dependency laws and same-input call domains in NBN_U03/U04/U05 and NBN_ResultDependency. No new LPh-style field stub/theorem/domain pair is required.

DigestEncodeRaw: forall eight-u32 digests, exact ok32-byte canonical little-endian image (not an input-canonicality assumption). DigestEncodeCanonical: same full ok law only when caller proves all eight limbs canonical. DigestDecode: forall32-byte arrays, exact full emitted `decode_digest_canonical` Result including every rejection. TransferEncode/WithdrawalEncode: forall typed records of that variant, exact full emitted encoder Result. Each is explicitly a lower U00–02 bridge still to be supplied; the source-model summaries prove their enclosing associations under precisely those names. No LPb drifted-lock stage is imported as a native proof.
