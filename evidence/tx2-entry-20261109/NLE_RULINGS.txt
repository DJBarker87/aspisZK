### Lead ruling (bc), 2026-11-09: extractor mistranslates explicit returns after a loop (batch_tree); emission-fidelity audit

(1) Finding (lead, from NKB-F1/NKL_). The pinned Aeneas emission of the ACTUAL frozen candidate.rs batch_tree (evidence/batch-merkle-20261108/NJS_emitted/raw/NJS_Extract/Funs.lean, verifier.candidate.batch_tree_loop.body and verifier.candidate.batch_tree) is not faithful. Frozen Rust, after the while loop: `node_count(..).ok_or(Error::Merkle)?`, `return Err(Error::Parse)` on the count/padding guard, `return Err(Error::Merkle)` when verify_in_place is false, then `Ok(())`. The emission folds this post-loop code into the loop's exit branch and encodes the three error exits as `ok (done (idx, val, none))`; the caller then maps `none` to `fail panic`.
