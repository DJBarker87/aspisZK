### Lead ruling (bc), 2026-11-09: extractor mistranslates explicit returns after a loop (batch_tree); emission-fidelity audit

(1) Finding (lead, from NKB-F1/NKL_). The pinned Aeneas emission of the ACTUAL frozen candidate.rs batch_tree (evidence/batch-merkle-20261108/NJS_emitted/raw/NJS_Extract/Funs.lean, verifier.candidate.batch_tree_loop.body and verifier.candidate.batch_tree) is not faithful. Frozen Rust, after the while loop: `node_count(..).ok_or(Error::Merkle)?`, `return Err(Error::Parse)` on the count/padding guard, `return Err(Error::Merkle)` when verify_in_place is false, then `Ok(())`. The emission folds this post-loop code into the loop's exit branch and encodes the three error exits as `ok (done (idx, val, none))`; the caller then maps `none` to `fail panic`. The tail `Ok(())` and the in-loop duplicate `return Err(Error::Merkle)` are emitted correctly as `some (...)`. The defect is therefore specific: an explicit `return` or `?` in code that runs after a loop, merged into the loop exit, loses its value. verify_in_place (tail expression only after its loop) and canonical_scan (tail `Ok(())` only; its `?` exits are inside the loop) do not show it on inspection.

(2) Classification. This is a TOOLING finding against the accepted extraction trust item ((ao)(3)(a), narrowed D188), not a protocol or Rust defect. The item is RECLASSIFIED, not removed: "Aeneas emission is trusted as faithful except for explicit returns or `?` in post-loop code merged into a loop exit; every emitted body relied on by a CLOSED or candidate row must be shown free of that pattern, or the affected exits must be certified natively against the frozen body." Reported to the owner as a trust-inventory narrowing.

(3) Audit (job NKT_, cx-emission-audit). For every emitted function body that any CLOSED row of evidence/ledger19-20261108 or any pending candidate relies on, decide from the frozen source and the emitted Lean whether a post-loop explicit return or `?` exists and whether its value is preserved. Output the affected list. A CLOSED row whose proved Result depends on a mistranslated exit is REOPENED with the exact exit named (accuracy does not need permission); a row whose theorem is insensitive to it (e.g. the exit is proved unreachable on the row's domain from the frozen source) stays CLOSED with that argument recorded.

(4) batch_tree route. Because the frozen emission is unfaithful, (ah) applies to batch_tree (eligibility of (ay)(2) extended to "emitted but shown unfaithful on named exits"). The model side is a Lean transcription of an explicit-Result f_ref with a line-by-line correspondence (no extraction needed; NKL_ E001/E002 references are retired); the native side is (ay)(3) segment contracts of the ACTUAL frozen body against f_ref at every exit (Kani reads the real MIR); then the (bb)(2)(b) transport to the cohort. ReviewedTranscription item NKL-RT-batch_tree. Target not yet established.
