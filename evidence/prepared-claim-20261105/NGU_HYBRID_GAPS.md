# Why the hybrid caller certificate is still OPEN

The exact canonical model law is CHECKED. The historical KNF/LPt native packet
is not a complete current local-record certificate: NGJ suspends seven of its
native fragments. NGU supplies17 matched local fragment families and original
KMX fidelity controls, and cites the CLOSED NFG/Q child packets. None of this
licenses a complete ruling(y) caller certificate without the association below.

Missing own-scope native deliverable: one arbitrary-prestate native inner-point
iteration on the SAME actual `Verified`, selected y1, g and complete `EbyKDot`
prestate (`c0.sums`, `c1.sums`, `count`). Its child oracle must record exact
selected value/scalar and full Dot prestate, return both `Result<(),()>` arms,
and apply an unconstrained full Dot poststate restricted to the child's modifies
set. The caller must produce one explicit G for its Result/control/ordered log,
including Parse on error and the exact untouched sem/y1/g frame. The matched
control must corrupt a recorded argument or reply, as ruling(y) requires.

NGU's separate selection, g-frame, `?`/Parse, advance, constant-control and
weighted-return checks do not jointly prove that G. Their inverted assertion
controls are valid local fragment controls, not a corruption control for an
unconstructed complete oracle log. The actual Verified-carrier cut quantifies
all read matrix cells, but fixes other fields; a full native representation/
frame association must justify that extension, rather than silently claiming
those fixed fields cover every caller. The pure raw-matrix selection gate does
not substitute for that native carrier association.

The next worker can instantiate the already checked NGU_LPm_PublicPort.source
and NGU_LPm_PointLoop.source with the CLOSED Dot child and thread the logged
poststate with NGU_LPq_PointConstruct's invariant. The direct canonical model,
byte windows, powers carrier and weighted arithmetic/induction need no legacy
kit and no multiplication replay. An extracted/replayed G-to-model association
is still required for the newly defined native G; it is not replaced with a
whole native output-equality hypothesis.

Caller-owned producer obligations remain independently OPEN: SAME native
semantic finish/dictionary (S/NFW); SAME compact464 check and29-row expansion
preserved to y1 (NFU/NGO); scalar/view and SAME powers29 argument association
(NGN/NGT/view owner). These are listed separately in NGU_EDGES.json.

All40 native allocations are used (38 verification invocations, two own-input
exclusions). No field product, whole-buffer native loop, timeout or memory-killed
