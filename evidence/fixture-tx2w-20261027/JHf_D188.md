# Narrowed native fidelity boundary

JHf_Scan source executes the actual retained afterstate decoder/encoder definitions and checks full byte identity. It does not establish D188 credit for those bodies. Its encoder depends on mutable range access/copy/back stores and the frontier loop. JHf_ProofWindow uses symbolic list slicing; it does not emulate a native copy. Authentication still depends on master/checkpoint iter_mut sequence writes and lane/frontier writes; zero sequence values cannot test restoration of nonzero writes. Query/flush, sampler/weights and finish retain inherited mutable-accessor boundaries. Emission inherits result codec mutable range/copy/back stores and syscall association. No native implementation-fidelity claim follows solely from fixture model success.
