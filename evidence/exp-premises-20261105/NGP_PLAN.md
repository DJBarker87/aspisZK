Mechanical work after packet delivery: byte-range equalities, handle/key
transport, same-state substitutions, concatenation of ordered logs, KNE
writer reuse, persistence data projection, nonempty792/1024 arithmetic and
existing lazyMean monotonicity. Native driver/callee domains and transcript
sampling association are substantive open proofs, not an expected one-line
composition. We do not estimate days without the actual native cut certificates.

Decisions to flag, not take:

1. **Physical scope of L159.** The adopted headline has an admitted verifier
account runtime; the Pool consumer is an extra physical realization path.
Lead/owner must confirm the reporting boundary: either prove Pool B/D into
that same admitted game (recommended completion route), or explicitly report
the headline as conditional on that external consumer association. Removing
Pool settlement from the security claim without saying so changes scope;
no such choice is made here.
2. **Clock projection.** Locate existing accepted realization or approve exact
NGP-R1 and list it as additional trust; alternatively prove the pinned
sysvar/runtime implementation. It cannot be silently absorbed by hash-only
RuntimeSyscall. No strict slot/epoch monotonicity is needed or proposed.
3. **Partial terminal policy.** Current model permits fail/divergence and
retains ideal suffixes. Prove its existing pointwise domination for physical
terminals. A totality restriction or changing abort probability would require
owner review if it changes the game/bound. We recommend the current partial
route and have NOT selected or imposed any restriction.
4. **Any genuine adaptive schedule mismatch.** If physical exporter reveals a
reorder not covered by ARX/F3S, prove a coupling/commutation under its actual
hypotheses, or return a concrete mismatch to the lead. Changing frozen3 or
privacy/event observables is an owner design decision, not a wrapper lemma.

No new security axiom, bound, collision allowance, adversary restriction,
privacy claim or two-transaction change. Prefix equality alone is insufficient
for Mass_statement. Completing both premises still requires the open drivers,
coherent release replay L187 and final trust/axiom/artifact audit L188.
