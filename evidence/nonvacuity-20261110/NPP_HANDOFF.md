## 3. Step 1: how the computable sdk is built

- **Cone.** The walk starts at `AspisR1.NOC_Run5bCtx.sdk` and is restricted to candidates: the 718 NPO_
  id-dependents together with the 58 noncomputable constants of NPO_L035. It gives 62 dependents in
  post-order. These are the 12 leaves, the 46 inherited definitions, and 4 id constants on the D path
  (NHJ_IdPacket.pool/registry, LIB_Deployed.ids/deployed). There is no theorem, inductive or unsafe
  dependent.
- **Leaves.** The 12 leaves contain 18 `Classical.propDecidable p` sites. Each site's refinement is a
  structural instance:
  - instance search at `p`;
  - else at `p` with the equality carrier unfolded (`whnfR`, then `whnf`). Aeneas `Array`/`Pubkey`/`Key`
    are `def`s over a Subtype, so they need this unfolding to reach `Subtype`/`List`/`UScalar`
    DecidableEq;
  - else at `p` delta-unfolded. `OraclePortFrozenOrder.wellFormed` needs this.

  A candidate instance is rejected if it mentions Classical or a noncomputable constant. One site, equality on
  `Stage2Finish.E = WideExact`, needed new instances: `deriving instance DecidableEq` for CM31, QM31 and
  WideExact. M31 already had one.
- **Equations.** A site is abstracted as `h xs : Decidable p` and proved equal by `Subsingleton.elim`
  at the Pi type. A dependency is abstracted as `.const y us` and proved equal by y's own equation. The
  equation is the congruence chain `F lhs = F rhs`, stated as `copy = reference`. The kernel checks it
  by delta and beta only, with no evaluation (AGENTS rule 2).
  - Stage A references the originals: 50 equations.
  - Stage B is a native tool, because importing NPO_CloneNov costs about 7 GB, which `lean -M7000`
    cannot hold (NPO_L017). It references `NPO_Nov.*` for the 12 id-dependent constants: `nov_eq`.
- **Ids.** The copies substitute CPH_Program.verifier, poolDefault and registryDefault with
  NPO_DeployedIds.{verifier,pool,registry}Nov, exactly as NPO_Clone did. That is why the stage-B
  equations close against the NPO_Nov constants.
