## Scope and provenance standard

“Installed” means a named Lean source/component interpretation, not an independently established deployed binary correspondence. Existing extraction headers and retained manifests identify provenance; D188-P/P2 association obligations remain. Residual records are data parameters, not correctness assumptions. No equality to a live deployment is invented.
