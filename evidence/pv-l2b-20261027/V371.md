# V371 dependency boundary

The Lean rejection/count/cursor identities, ideal IID probability algebra,
entropy short-circuit class preservation, and honest guard algebra are
mathematical source-level claims. Their statements do not assume DOITM or
V371 and do not conclude anything about instruction latency.

Transferring them to physical timing distributions still requires the
existing substantive V371 item, under the D248 decision that DOITM is OFF:

* Seed expansion uses private hash inputs, byte loads/conversion, AND and
  comparisons. Equal rejection histories do not establish equal latency
  for different accepted words or hash states.
* Entropy's fixed 32-byte OR scan still processes private operands. Branch
  class simulation does not prove data-independent OR/compare latency or
  the getrandom/provider/OS schedule law.
* N2/N4/N5 sum and balance code processes secret field operands, including
  addition, subtraction and comparisons. A constant guard outcome does not
  settle the timing of the operands that produced it.
* N3 canonicality/round-trip conversion processes private limbs. Successful
  guards alone do not settle conversion/compare latency.
* N6 interpolation/oracle/evaluation uses private field arithmetic. The
  boundary/terminal outcome may be constant while these operations retain
  V371. Public interpolation denominators do not remove that dependency.

V371 is necessary for these instruction-timing transfers; this is not a
claim that it is sufficient for OS effects, cache behavior, executable/source
binding, or complete observer composition. No trust field is added. The
allowed trust record remains exactly entropy_law, entropy_kernel, observation.
