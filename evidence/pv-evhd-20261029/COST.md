The model-layer delta is modest (four small experimental modules), but the
native **joint** trace/timing obligations are substantive. A rough planning
range is 3–8 person-weeks of integration and joint-model proof work, overlapping
with current owners, plus unfinished native leaves. The rows overlap and must
not simply be summed. No reliable whole-target ETA follows from this range.

Option (2), retaining the current headline and composing separate side-channel
results, still needs the same joint context/timing, signal and causality links.
Marginal results alone do not reduce that cost. Option (1) makes the required
carrier explicit and removes the impossible Result-fibre installation task;
it does not prove the new native links automatically.

Under EXACT D163/D166/public-failure links, the old bound is retained verbatim
and no extra timing/trace term is mathematically forced. If only approximate
simulation is available, the appropriate displayed statement is the old bound
plus an explicitly bounded joint trace term; its old <2^-100 conclusion does
not transfer automatically. Neither the exact native links nor an approximate
native error bound is established by this experimental draft.
