query, flush, writer and original-writer bridge modules remain imported.

explicit mathematical SHA-realization hypothesis allowed by the worker task
when the unconditional certificate/SDK import union exceeds memory. Its
inhabitant is to be checked separately from the SDK cone using retained SHA
certificates. Until that check and the parent/wrapper check pass, neither is
