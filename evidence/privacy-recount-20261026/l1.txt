## Intended theorem and source boundary

First derive ordered, bounded traces from the ACTUAL
ProverRwRngCallerReplay.Replay relation. A step at index 8 checks the cap but
enters no callback; indices below 8 are the entered attempts. Each payload must
come from a separately identified native callback relation, not be invented
from the final output. cap8 and ordered_indices provide control/order, not
body/IO/TLS payloads. universal_cap16 supplies the entropy-owner same-event
certificate, but its provider/error sorts require native installation.

The intended native theorem equates complete ordered observations of extracted
execution and the L1 reference. Missing callback facts must be named Prop
fields in PV_L1_Premises, one field for each targeted ledger row. These remain
proof obligations, not definitions of native behavior or cryptographic axioms.
No premise-removal claim follows from packaging them.

For V348/V366, use one sample space and the headline's existing realBad event.
A relational transition invariant must preserve publication, masks, retry
origins, oracle history, TLS, ledger and raw inputs together. Prove lockstep
prefix transport before a TV bound. Equal-circle, QueryScan abort/observer
prefixes and part47 byte encoding must preserve that SAME invariant. Do not
replace simE or add separate charges for previously charged events.
