TX1:084 OPEN, slice boundary for the canonical7/7/64 tail. No native whole-buffer scan or SHA computation is attempted.

The first four literal frozen calls are checked against the source in NIY_SCAN_ASSOCIATION.json. The local prefix caller uses unconstrained full8-error child replies plus an unconstrained tail Result, and checks exact slice descriptors/modes, call count/order and full Result. It is a prefix observation, not a full native scan packet. One actual M31 loop iteration includes the range exit and exact Parse/Ok behavior.
