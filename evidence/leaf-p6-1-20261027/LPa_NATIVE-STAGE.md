# Direct library bridge stage

The seven changed shipped source files contain cfg(kani)-only harness modules.
All original computational bodies are unchanged from frozen revision4bb3b3f51.
LPa_source-provenance.json records their entry/frozen equality. Native library
functions are called, not copied or replaced by stubs. The safe references are
independent source-stage models corresponding to XRe/XRf/XRg references.

One private Prefix constructor exists only under cfg(kani), for modeled carrier
experiments; this is not a proof that arbitrary Prefix records passed parsing.
Claims sparse experiments do not establish general87-lane/allocator refinement.
Circle stage uses a zero block known to reach the fallback; arbitrary sampler
arithmetic and closure extraction remain separate. Dot shares the native raw
producer in its fold-stage check; XRk_raw arithmetic association remains open.

All20 Kani allocations are serial through unchanged S2H/S2W runners. Host
compile and verification phases are optimized. ELF rebuild/pin comparison is
required by the cfg-only source changes and is not yet complete at this commit.
