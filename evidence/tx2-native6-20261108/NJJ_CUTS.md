These are source-call cuts, not separate native std producer rows.

Its native test uses proof57607 and public length0..1880; applying it outside that parent-established scope requires an additional domain/projection proof, never a stronger root hypothesis.

prepared_tail and accumulator_tail split the source's final pair construction. Both have separate corrupt-value and different-allocation controls. NJJ_Constructors assembles their complete value carriers. No timed-out combined-constructor shape is recreated.

Controls corrupt an Ok payload, map the wrong error, and change the child argument. The oracles are CLOSED-child boundaries under (y); their tag is not itself proof of the runtime allocator event.
