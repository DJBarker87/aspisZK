# F1 and F2 conclusions

## F1

The adopted failure event is accepted extraction failure for ONE first-submission
transcript across a TX1/TX2 split. ARQ_AccountView.physical, SCE.submission,
F3T_ByteCarrier.lift, F3T_Charged.charged and F3T_Prefix.first_start_before_terminal
retain exactly that transcript. Its decoder-start upper bound is Q0+34, and
therefore Q0+Q1+34. No +68 is needed for that event. This is an upper bound,
not a claim that 34 distinct starts are attained on every submission.

Adaptive choice work is separate: Q must include the actual reached second
adversary phase. ART_AdversaryWork.work / phases and ART_FullBudget.issued
prove Q+1921 on every partial outcome, from 1018 first-wrapper plus 903
second-wrapper calls. ART_FullCharge gives Q+2965 (1921+1044) audited reads.
No second-choice query or rejected prefix is deleted. Rc10's existing first-only
queries binder does NOT establish this full-work budget; ARS_CostObstruction
remains valid. Correcting the quantified work domain is essential, and is not
permission to silently alter the root's current budget antecedent.

At Q=0, retained full total <2^-100. Classifier term exactly255/256 of target;
margin exactly2^-108 -6(1161²+2965²)/2^256 -2^-126 -2^-454 >0. If the event
were TWO independently submitted transcripts, 68 starts alone would exceed
2^-100 by127*2^-107. That alternate event is not adopted or substituted.

## F2

ART_PartialProgram preserves actual syscall status, raw attempted log, ordered
addresses, repeated calls and abort. It never follows a failing syscall's
ideal continuation. ART_QueriedCoverage.original proves representation for
EVERY a and EVERY H agreeing with the native execution on its queried addresses,
including failed-attempt addresses. Original partial native wrapper outcomes
and original total attempted cost are identical under the retained extraction
boundary; the shared choice programs are unchanged. ART_QueriedPieces preserves
the four chronology pieces whenever TX1 succeeded; TX2 may reject.

ART_AccountAccepted / ART_RuntimeAccepted prove accepted runs equal the old
ideal account in every H. ART_Runtime.rejected / ART_RejectedNative.mass prove
rejected runs have zero failure mass without erasing their cost.
ART_RuntimeDomination adds ZERO loss under averaging. Thus F2 is closed at
source/native/experiment rejection representation. It is not an all-world
bank-support or release theorem.

## Remaining composition obstruction

ART_QueryRestricted.installed and ART_NoFactory.impossible are conditional on
the full installed native tuple plus a returning syscall at an unissued
address. Change only that address: the same accepted execution and bank
SupportedEntry persist, but ARQ_WorldSupport.NativeAt and its all-world factory
cannot hold. Even an existential new sha256 cannot help: its P1 is GLOBAL and
RuntimeSyscall constrains EVERY returning call. This checked obstruction is at
the old interface, not the correctness of a query-only replacement. It does
not refute an actual 100-bit protocol guarantee.

ART_CylinderDomination / ART_CylinderRecovery compose the source representation
with ARQ and SCF/SCI/SCJ consumers at native on the RHS. The new H may have no
global P1. Replacing that RHS with the same H under the unconditioned ROM law
is still unproved. The finite-mixture law transports original supplied support;
it never asserts a fixed native cylinder has unconditional probability one.

The next deliverable is a queried-address generalization of installed source/
cut/recovery consumers and a bank-backed original-law execution factory across
different H, followed by actual Rc10 replacement with full adaptive-work metric.
No new cryptographic/runtime trust assertion is adopted to hide this obligation.
