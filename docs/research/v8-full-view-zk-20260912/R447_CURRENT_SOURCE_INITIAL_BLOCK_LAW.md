# R442–R447: source-shaped initial-block limb law

This milestone combines the finite rejection model with a bounded source-shaped first-limb result. R442 encodes the rejection alphabet as `Option (Fin p)`. R443 proves exact first-accepted failure and value masses on a finite uniform option tape. R445 transports that law to the formal model of one uniform 32-byte block, split into eight masked 31-bit words. R446 relates the recursive tape scan to list scanning and the numeric sentinel representation.

R444 proves the bounded `limbRun` facts for eight words in the current block: no refill occurs under the stated index bound, and the scan’s result and consumed cursor offset match the source-shaped sentinel scan. R447 then proves, for every `H` and source state `s`, that the initial `limbRun` result has failure mass `(1 / 2^31)^8`, and each `a : Fin (2^31 - 1)` has mass `(1 - (1 / 2^31)^8) / (2^31 - 1)`, when averaged over the formal uniform block model.

Their successful compile runs and all retained earlier drafts are indexed in [the evidence bundle](evidence/r447-source-initial-block-law/README.md).

Each successful target has a complete saved `#print axioms` report using only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. R442 compiled in 1.27 s, R443 in 1.59 s, R445 in 1.31 s, R446 in 1.30 s, R444’s canonical target in 1.72 s, and R447 in 1.38 s; swap was zero for each. R444 has an earlier successful scratch-module compile as well as the canonical qualified-target compile; only the latter is the downstream module target.

The established boundary is the first limb over one formal uniform block. It does not establish refill behavior or the whole four-limb distribution under the actual shared oracle, nor IID/freshness or full privacy/security. The next obligation is the conditional refill law and the coupling of all four limbs to the actual shared-oracle probability model.
