It uses the existing exact little-endian block/31-bit split from R421, retains the ignored high bits in an explicit bijection, and then flattens the four banks of eight low words in source order. `blockTape_value` identifies each decoded tape entry with the actual source-shaped masked little-endian word. No enumeration of the large field or block alphabet is used.

`four_block_projection` transports every rational observation of that tape. `four_block_tuple_mass` then gives the exact success mass for any ordered four-limb target in the sequential eight-attempt-per-limb word program: `((1-(1/2147483648)^8)/2147483647)^4`. Stopping and cursor movement remain those of R572/R578; this does not allocate separate fixed rejection rows to each limb.
