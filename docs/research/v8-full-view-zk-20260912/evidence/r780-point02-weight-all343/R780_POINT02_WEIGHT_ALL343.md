# R780 point0/point2 weight coverage package

This evidence bundle records successful focused Lean outputs for all 343 distinct `pointWeight` indices required by the pinned R748 plan, at both selected points 0 and 2. The successful outputs are source-shaped finite gather/value identities; the reports use only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. Exact targets, source revisions and checksums, wall time, peak RSS, swap, complete axiom output, import pins, and all attempts are recorded in `MANIFEST.json` and the archived per-run files.

The finite weight coverage does not by itself prove native callback execution, source-to-model correspondence, accepted-prefix coverage, privacy, soundness, shared-oracle challenge laws, a simulator, or an overall security bound. Failed attempts are retained, including the initial incorrect-import attempt and runs whose proof output contained `sorryAx`; only final exit-zero snapshots are in `verified-source/`.
