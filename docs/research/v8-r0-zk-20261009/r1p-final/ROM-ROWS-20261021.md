## Stage 2: binary grammar and observers

`R1Z.RomGrammarBin` proves a uniform length bound for every transcript row,
all eight commitment constructor families, and every D79 Tx1Class (including
57,607-byte compact proofs, ASF8 and checkpoint checksum bodies). The shared
binary grammar is imported through its established equality bridge. Leaf
payloads have at most 480 bytes; all public statement byte strings are at
most publicWidth. The chosen bound is
`57607 + publicWidth + semDomain.length + openDomain.length`.
No table, large finite universe or recurrence is evaluated by reduction.

`Record` includes private bindings/seeds/salts/expanders, binary roots and
internal nodes, salted leaves, transcript inputs, D79 classified records,
and public digest inputs. Every constructor decodes into the finite domain.
`ReadCoverage` requires the public width and typed membership of **every**
model read at all w/sec/d/v/p, including exhausted/default-tape and
inconsistent-answer vectors. `address_domain` proves RAddrIn from it;
`public_digest_domain` covers the frozen digest. Source membership is not
asserted, nor is Pos1 claimed to enumerate additional operational source
calls. R10/R-wire must establish the complete source inventory through both
transactions, backend, normalization, checkpoint and resume.

`R1Z.RomObserverBin` proves the frozen lazy-table uniformity law on the
chosen carrier, length ≤ fuel for every adaptive runLog, and ≤2^64 queries
for the chosen wire. `observerMap` copies the source's Boolean decision
function on the **entire** ROMView; both pointwise and fsR1P observer
preservation are proved. Only the operational query program is supplied;
source game/log correspondence still belongs to the two ScanCoupling facts.
