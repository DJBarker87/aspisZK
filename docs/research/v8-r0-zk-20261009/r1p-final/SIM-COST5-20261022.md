actual `AspisWideTower.WideExact` carrier. Addition costs exactly 10 ticks
(two QM31 additions at 4 each and two stores). Schoolbook multiplication
costs exactly 170 ticks (five QM31 products at 32, two additions at 4,
two stores). This implements the field law, rather than treating WideExact
as QM31. Binary powering costs at most `343*k+1`: two WideExact products

the resulting total inverse, including zero, at cost at most 85,065 ticks.

Rust instruction count or refinement of `wide_exact.rs::try_inv` (which uses
a norm). No shipped file was changed.
