1.1 `lanes` is a production relation, not a lane-map fact.
    lanes_iff_nonCopy (hcard : 4096 < Fintype.card K) B pub t
        (hA : BaseTyped F t) (hpub : PublicBase F pub) :
      (∀ λ χ i b, i < 28 → laneOf t pub λ χ B i b = 0) ↔ NonCopyHolds pub t
    where NonCopyHolds = Holds of value, occupancy, asset, schedule, path,
    digest and poseidonScalar (the seven λ/χ-free conjuncts of R0P
    ProductionHolds, SemAssembly.lean:29).  Proof: pick a pole-free χ
    (exists_noPole: `poleVal` from 2·2048 row-slot values into K is not
    surjective when |K| > 4096, Fintype.card_le_of_surjective), form the
    prepared instance `prepare pub t (chOf 0 χ)`, on which the Copy lane
    vanishes (copyHolds_prepare) and lanes 0..27 equal those of t
    (laneOf_prepare_lt); then the proved R0P equivalence
    lanes_zero_iff_holds (LaneMap.lean:817) on the prepared instance, and
    transport of every Holds back to t (holds_prepare, rowOpenings_prepare).
    laneOf_lt_indep: lanes 0..27 read neither λ nor χ.
    hcard is a field-size fact (|K| = p⁴ ≈ 2^124 for QM31), not a builder fact.

§2  The refinement premise (what is NOT proved)

    structure BuilderRefines (S : BuilderSpec F W) (Rel : Public K → W → Prop) : Prop
      nonCopy : ∀ pub w, Rel pub w → NonCopyHolds pub (S.build pub w)
      balance : ∀ pub w, Rel pub w → CopyLinkBalance pub (S.build pub w)

    Intended instantiation: W := Wspend, Rel pub w := Rspend (projectPublic
    pub) w (SpendRelation.lean:41 on the spend-path branch; PublicBase
    projection as in PublicTraceWitness.lean:8), S := the Rust builder
    compile_pool_v1_pair_forest_*_merged_c1_v1 → semantic_c1 (c1) and
    host_semantic::build_with_material (rest: H1/C2 columns; their values
    are irrelevant to all three fields, since `prepare` overwrites column 26
    and no field reads columns 27-28).  The instantiation is not made on
    this branch: SpendRelation and its imports (R1InputWitnessCheck,
    PublicOptionShape, AspisV8R15.ExactTowerBase, R1SpendAppendBinding) are
    not on the R0Z build path; W and Rel are parameters, so the theorems
    apply verbatim once the modules share a build.

    Rust status of the two premises: the honest compiler runs a host
    residual check (aspis-prover pool_v1_pair_forest_honest.rs:80 `finish`:
    exactly 18,089 transfer residuals, all zero; aspis-statement
    pair_forest_constraint_residuals.rs:79) that includes Poseidon,
    schedule, copy-alias, path, value, occupancy, append and public
    residuals.  That is a RUNTIME check on each build, not a proof for all
    witnesses, and its correspondence to the Lean families (NonCopyHolds)
    and to CopyLinkBalance over R0P copyLinks is not proved.  The refinement
    obligation is therefore: for every w with Rspend, the Rust builder's
    columns satisfy the Lean families and link balance.  It can be
    discharged either (a) by a Lean model of the builder (the column-wise
    definitions of the legacy pair-trace builder, relocate_and_extend and
    bank merge), proving NonCopyHolds/CopyLinkBalance per family, or (b) by
    an Aeneas/extraction refinement of the Rust functions to such a model.

    Why nothing more follows from Rspend alone: with S abstract, Rspend
    constrains w, not S.build pub w.  Any S whose build is a trace with a
    nonzero non-copy residual (e.g. Poseidon.lean:233's committed trace)
    satisfies the types and violates nonCopy, for every w.  So neither
    nonCopy nor balance is derivable from Rspend without a concrete
    builder; (a) is the honest-completeness proof that would replace the
    premise.
