DESIGN-T2b-2b — proof design for the spanning half of R0's privacy theorem
==========================================================================

Job T2b-2b-D (design only; no Lean written, no host run).  Base: v8-reference
0f25a792f.  Written against LOG decisions D17, D19, D20, D20′, D20″, D20‴,
D20⁗, T2b-2a′, T2b-2a″, the accepted T2b-1 and T2b-2a modules, and the
numeric record Z4c/Z5/Z7/Z8/Z8b.

Target in one line: on P_final ch (Good₃ plus named conditions, §4), every K₄-valued element of Chain0 ch is the
payload of a silent mask tape, i.e.
    Chain0₄ ch ⊆ ((A h.transport B ch).comp inlM) '' silentMasks h.transport B ch.

Headline findings of this design (details in the sections named):
  F1 (§2.1)  The target follows from two statements, neither of which is the
     modular (P)/(Q) lemma that D20⁗ refuted:
       (Q′) R(ker own) ⊇ Chain_T0₄   (rounds are spanned by tapes silent in
            every per-lane observation; initial sum left free), and
       (B)  bat(ker own ∩ ker R) = bat(ker own)   (the batched opening
            coordinates impose no condition on the rounds).
     The glue is structural linear algebra plus maskSum = initial sum.
  F2 (§2.2)  The left kernel of the observations on own-silent tapes is
     exactly the verifier's own linear checks: R0's `round_eval` (1 K-relation),
     `round_boundary` (1), and FRI fibre consistency `fold_encoded` at each of
     the 22 queried fibres (22).  24 K-relations = 192 limbs, which is exactly
     Z8's 2,112 − 1,920 and Z8b's 2,488 − 2,296.
  F3 (§2.5)  D20⁗'s 300-limb (P) shortfall is explained structurally: on D's
     coefficients 56–1023 with zero OOD values the chord quotient is divisible
     by x^28, so its fold vanishes on the 14 low fibres d < 14 (fibre d reads
     coefficients 4d..4d+3).  The resulting count reproduces Z8 exactly
     (1,920) and Z8b to within 8 limbs (1,804 predicted vs 1,812 measured;
     residual unexplained, in the harmless direction).  The low fibres must
     be fed by a lane with low-coefficient freedom inside ker R (proposed: G).
  F4 (§2.2, §3)  MODEL GAP.  Every mask lane in Lean is balanced at row 0
     against copyInactiveRows (`maskOnlyColumns`, `dWord`, and G in
     `applyAff`), so each lane's inactive-row sum is 0 and `.inactiveSum` ≡ 0
     on mask tapes.  Z8 and Z8b used UNBALANCED mask-only/G/D row sources
     (z8.rs `pure_sources`), a strict superset of the Lean mask space; Z7 kept
     the per-lane inactive entry but used the QM31 batching proxy.  No probe
     has run the proved protocol's payload on the Lean mask space.  A small
     probe Z8c (§3.3) is required before D21.
  F5 (§2.4)  The own-silence of lanes with exponent e ≥ 14 and of G is NOT
     round-free: the claim at the xor12 point is a function of the lane's
     round-6 polynomial (the 16 round-6 classes are singletons and
     2·8 ≤ e+2), so per-lane own-silence imposes one extra round-6 condition
     on those lanes.  Any per-lane transversality lemma must carry this.
  F6 (§2.3, §4)  The proof route needs Λ₄ = K₄ (fresh mask-lane generators at
     rounds 4–7), which D20‴'s Good₂ (span of 1, α₀..α₅ — giving only
     Λ₆ = Λ₇ = K₄) does not imply.  Proposed predicate
       Good₃ := Good ∧ (∀ i, ch.sem i ∈ K₄) ∧ at least three of α₀..α₃ ∉ K₂,
     which implies Λ_j = K₄ for all j ≥ 4 by a stabiliser argument, with
     failure mass ≤ 6·((1+δ_Q)/P²)² ≈ 2^-121.4 plus the 21-event part.
     D20‴'s Good₂′ (∃ i < 7, α_i ∉ K₂) is not sufficient for this route
     (it gives a two-dimensional Λ₇ only); whether the statement itself holds
     under Good₂′ is an open numeric question (§3.3 (K)/(L)).
  F7 (§2.4)  The remaining non-structural content is transversality of the
     opened-symbol functionals at the queried fibres against the row-side
     round kernels of the mask lanes and G (OT-open).  I have no proof route
     with a usable bad-set density for it; it is the main risk.

§1  Target in Lean vocabulary
-----------------------------
§1.1  K₄.  PackBasis F (R0P/CoreExt.lean:31) carries only i, u and F-linear
independence of {1, i, u, iu}; it does not say that their span is closed under
multiplication.  The spanning proof multiplies K₄ values constantly (eqPrefix,
β powers, tower factors times words, (α₉ − ½)⁻¹ in sRem), so closure is a
real premise.  Proposal (new module R0Z/K4.lean):

  def PackField (B : PackBasis F) : Prop :=
    B.i * B.i ∈ F ∧ ∃ a b : K, a ∈ F ∧ b ∈ F ∧ B.u * B.u = a + B.i * b
    -- QM31 tower: i² = −1, u² = 2 + i; a refinement fact of the concrete B.

  def K4set (B) : Set K := {x | ∃ v : Fin 4 → K, (∀ j, v j ∈ F) ∧ x = pack4 B v}
  def K4 (B) (hB : PackField B) : Subfield K   -- carrier K4set B
  def K2 (B) (hB) : Subfield K                 -- carrier {a + B.i * b | a b ∈ F}

Closure facts to prove (all STRUCTURAL, small): zero/one/add/neg/mul (the six
products of basis elements reduce by the two PackField relations); inv
(a nonzero x ∈ K4set: left multiplication is an injective F-linear map of the
4-dimensional K4set, hence surjective; or x⁻¹ = x^(|K4set|−2)); F ⊆ K4;
B.i, B.u, towerBasis B j ∈ K4; pack4 B v ∈ K4 for F-valued v; the casts of
naturals and (1/2 : K) lie in K4; K2 ≤ K4; F-independence (pack4_eq_zero_iff)
gives finrank_F K4 = 4 and finrank_F K2 = 2.  Recommended alternative
definition: IntermediateField.adjoin F {B.i, B.u}, with the lemma
mem_K4_iff_pack4 under PackField; either is fine, but the carrier
characterisation through pack4 is what every word lemma uses.

Two field facts beyond K₄ itself are used, and should be premises of the
final theorem rather than derived for abstract K:
  (K-deg)   ∀ x : K, ∃ a b ∈ K4, x = a + ch.opening 3 * b
            (true iff [K:K₄] = 2 and α_f ∉ K₄; WideExact has [K:K₄] = 2, so this
            is the CONDITIONAL event α_f ∉ K₄ of §4);
  (rot)     for α ∉ K₂ and 0 ≠ τ ∈ K₄: span_F{τ, iτ}·span_F{1, α} = K₄
            (STRUCTURAL from finrank_{K₂} K₄ = 2).

§1.2  The predicate.  As fixed in D20‴.2 (Lean form):

  def Good2 (B) (hB) (ch : Challenges K) : Prop :=
    Good ch ∧ (∀ i : Fin 25, ch.sem i ∈ K4 B hB) ∧
    ∀ x ∈ K4 B hB, x ∈ Submodule.span F
      (Set.insert 1 (Set.range fun i : Fin 6 => alpha ch ⟨i, by omega⟩))

(Submodule.span F over the Subfield F acting on K; stated as an inclusion so no
K4-as-F-submodule coercion is needed.)  This gives Λ₆ = Λ₇ = K₄ only.  The
route of §2.3 needs Λ₄ = K₄, so the proposed predicate is

  def Good3 (B) (hB) (ch) : Prop :=
    Good ch ∧ (∀ i : Fin 25, ch.sem i ∈ K4 B hB) ∧
    ∃ s : Finset (Fin 4), 3 ≤ s.card ∧ ∀ i ∈ s, alpha ch ⟨i, by omega⟩ ∉ K2 B hB

Lemma lambda4_eq_K4 (STRUCTURAL): Good3 → Λ₄ = K₄ (hence Λ_j = K₄ for
j ≥ 4, as Λ_{j+1} ⊇ Λ_j).  Proof: take i, m, k in s.  A_i := span_F{1, α_i}
has stabiliser F (a larger stabiliser would be K₂ or K₄, making A_i a K₂-line
through 1, i.e. A_i = K₂ ∋ α_i).  So α_k·A_i ≠ A_i and V := A_i + α_k·A_i
has dimension ≥ 3; if 3, α_m·V ≠ V (else V is an F(α_m) = K₄-subspace), and
two distinct 3-dimensional subspaces of K₄ span it.  Λ₄ contains the
span of the products A_i·A_k·A_m (eqPrefix over coordinates 0..3 spans all
multilinear monomials in α₀..α₃).  §4 gives the density.

Additional CONDITIONAL hypotheses that the global rank needs (§2.4–2.5) are
collected in §4 as GoodOpen ch (circle points, opening scalars, queries).  The
final predicate is P_final := Good₃ ∧ GoodOpen ∧ GoodGeneric, where
GoodGeneric is the set of polynomial non-vanishing conditions of §2.4/§2.5;
§4 gives each with its density.

§1.3  The target space.

  def Chain0K4 (B) (hB) (ch) : Submodule F (Payload K) where
    carrier := {v | v ∈ Chain0 ch ∧ ∀ cell, v cell ∈ K4 B hB}

(Chain0 ch is a K-submodule; restrictScalars F, intersected with the
K₄-valued cells, which form an F-submodule because K4 is closed under F-linear

§1.4  The theorem (T2b-2b).

  theorem chain0K4_le_silent (hB : PackField B) (hπ : D13Order h.transport)

§2  Argument: global kernel-rank structure
------------------------------------------
§2.1  Reduction to (Q′) and (B)

Write, for a mask tape m (all maps F-linear on M K F):
  R m      ∈ RoundPayload K          (the ten round polynomials),
  own m    := (own_ℓ (u_ℓ))_ℓ        (per-lane cells, lanes 16..25, 27, 28),
  bat m    := (.openingCoeff i, .finalCoeff d) coordinates,
  ms m     := .maskSum,  ia m := .inactiveSum.

Facts already proved or STRUCTURAL:
  (a) ia ≡ 0 on M (each balanced lane has σ_ℓ = 0; §2.2.1).
  (b) ms m = p₀(0) + p₀(1) of R m (L_maskSum + rounds_initial, as in the proof
      of silent_mem_chain0).
  (c) R m satisfies the nine chain equalities (rounds_chain) and
      evalRound9(R m) = claimMask(own m) (Phi0_A); on ker own the terminal is
      0, so R(ker own) ⊆ Chain_T0.
  (d) m ∈ silentMasks ⇔ ms m = 0 ∧ own m = 0 ∧ ia m = 0 ∧ bat m = 0
      (mem_silentMasks_iff, grouping its eight clauses).

Statements to prove:
  (Q′)  ∀ t ∈ Chain_T0₄, ∃ x ∈ ker own, R x = t.
        (Chain_T0₄ := K₄-valued round vectors with the nine chain equalities
        and p₉(α₉) = 0, initial sum free; F-dim 4·270 = 1,080.)
  (B)   bat(ker own ∩ ker R) = bat(ker own).

Lemma (reduction; STRUCTURAL).  (Q′) ∧ (B) → Chain0K4 ≤ silent image.
Proof.  Let v ∈ Chain0K4; its round part t lies in Chain_T0₄ with initial sum
0.  By (Q′) pick x ∈ ker own with R x = t.  By (B) pick y ∈ ker own ∩ ker R
with bat y = bat x.  Put m := x − y.  Then own m = 0, bat m = 0, ia m = 0 by
(a), R m = t, and ms m = p₀(0)+p₀(1) = 0 by (b) and t ∈ Chain0.  By (d)
m ∈ silentMasks, and A(inlM m) = roundInclusion t = v (the non-round cells of
v are 0 because v ∈ Chain0, and those of A(inlM m) are 0 by silence).  ∎

The lemma is pure linear algebra over F: a generic statement
  `exists_silent_of_cover {V W₁ W₂ W₃ : F-modules} (R : V → W₁) (o : V → W₂)
   (b : V → W₃) (T : Submodule) (hQ : T ≤ (ker o).map R)
   (hB : (ker o ⊓ ker R).map b = (ker o).map b) : T ≤ (ker o ⊓ ker b).map R`
and an instantiation that identifies own/bat/R/ms/ia with the payload cells.

Equivalent global form (for comparison with D20⁗'s wording).  Let
O := (own, bat, ms) on M.  Then (Q′) ∧ (B) is equivalent to
  O(ker R) = O(M) ∩ {ms = 0, claimMask = 0}  together with  R(M) ⊇ Chain_T0₄,
i.e. "the observations restricted to ker R have full rank modulo the two
couplings that the sumcheck itself imposes (initial sum ↔ maskSum, terminal ↔
claimMask)".  The split into (Q′) and (B) is preferred because own is
lane-separable — ker own = ⊕_ℓ ker own_ℓ, so (Q′) is a sum over lanes —
while bat is the only cross-lane block, and (B) can be proved with two
explicit absorbers (D-high and G-low, §2.5).

Why this is not D20‴'s modular split.  (Q) there was "own-silent kernels plus
D(0..55) span the rounds" — that is (Q′), and Z8b(2) supports it.  (P) was "D's
coefficients 56..1023 cancel all batched coordinates and D's own" — false
(§2.5 explains why: the 14 low fold fibres are unreachable from D-high).  (B)
replaces (P): it allows any own-silent round-kernel tape to cancel the batched
coordinates, and §2.5 names the two families that do it.

Numeric status of the reduction's inputs (details §3): Z8 (unbalanced
sources, WideExact batching) gives R(ker own ∩ ker bat) = 1,080 = Chain_T0
(initial retained), which implies (Q′) and (B) for the unbalanced model.  For
the Lean (balanced) model neither has been measured; Z8c item (2)/(4) does.

§2.2  Observation inventory, balancing, verifier relations, dimensions

§2.2.1  Balancing (STRUCTURAL; model correction F4).  applyAff writes lane
16+c as maskOnlyColumns = balance 0 (raw), lane 27 as balance 0 (pack4 raw G),
lane 28 as dWord = balance 0 (pack4 raw D); `balance 0` replaces row 0 (the
first inactive row) by minus the sum of the other inactive rows.  Hence
σ_ℓ(u_ℓ) = 0 for every mask lane and
  ia m = Σ_c γ^c σ_c(u_c) = 0                         (lemma ia_pure_zero)
for every m : M (the H1 lane is in N).  Consequences: .inactiveSum is not a
silence condition for mask tapes; the word space of each lane is the
hyperplane W_ℓ = ker σ_ℓ; every row source in a probe must be the balanced
e_r − e_0 (r ∈ I∖{0}) or e_r (r ∉ I).  Z8/Z8b used e_r for all r and imposed
only the batched ia = 0 (8 limbs) instead of the twelve per-lane σ_ℓ = 0 (18
limbs); their silent space is a superset of Lean's by up to 10 limbs, so
1,076 is an upper bound for Lean, not yet a measurement (§3.3).

§2.2.2  Per-lane own observations (row basis vs coefficient basis).
  claims_j(u) = Σ_r eqWeight(openingPoints α j)(r)·u r        row basis
      points: j=0 toR0 α; j=1 toR0 (successorPoint α); j=2 toR0 (xor12Point α)
  extra(u)    = Σ_{k<56} libraWeight(α)_k · c_k                 coefficient basis
  ood_i(u)    = evalMessage c z_i = p0(z_i.x) + z_i.y·p1(z_i.x)  coefficient basis
  opened(u)   = Enc(c)(childIndex q s), q ∈ queries            codeword
Limb counts on balanced words: F-lane 12 + 4 + 8 + 88 = 112; K₄-lane (G, D)
4·(3 + 1 + 2 + 88) = 376.  Total own: 10·112 + 2·376 = 1,872.

§2.2.3  The batched block.  With p_ℓ := c_ℓ, I_ℓ := interpolant z₀ z₁ (ood_ℓ),
and Q := quotientLinear z₀ z₁ (the chord quotient),
  q := batch = Σ_ℓ γ^ℓ · Q(c_ℓ) ∈ K^1024,   w := openingWeights π ch,
  .openingCoeff i = coeff_i (roundPolynomial q w)   (relationLinear = R0's
                     roundPolynomial up to the quarter; both carry it),
  .finalCoeff d   = foldMessage α_f q d = Σ_s α_f^s q(4d+s)   (foldMessage_eq_sum).
Over F these are 263 K-values = 2,104 limbs.

§2.2.4  The verifier's linear checks are the left kernel (F2).  Let
GoodCircle ch := z₀ ≠ z₁ ∧ ¬BaseRational z₀ ∧ ¬BaseRational z₁ (the last two
hold for every sampler output: circleSampleParameterWith_not_rational).  Under
GoodCircle, for every tape in ker own:
  (S1) (roundPolynomial q w).eval α_f = quarter·dot (foldMessage α_f q)
       (citedDualFold α_f w)                           [round_eval, all q, w]
  (S2) coeff 0 + coeff 4 = quarter·dot q w = 0          [round_boundary;
       dot q w = dot q qWeights + τ·e1 q + τ²·e2 q (FunctionalWeights.row_pairing);
       e1 = e2 = 0 on the quotient image (image_iff); dot (Q p) qWeights =
       dot (p − I) weights (image_pairing); weights pairs p into
       Σ_j κ^{j+1} claims_j + σ + κ⁴·extra, all 0 on ker own; I = 0 since
       ood = 0]
  (S3_u) for each queried fibre u:
       exactFinalEncoder (foldMessage α_f q) u = coefficientFoldValue α_f
       (channels (Enc q) · u) = 0                       [fold_encoded,
       encoded_channels; Enc q at the four children of u equals
       Σ_ℓ γ^ℓ (opened_ℓ − Enc I_ℓ)/L = 0, using no_domain_zero and
       chordMessage_encoder for "the quotient word is a codeword"]
That is 1 + 1 + 22 = 24 K-relations = 192 limbs.  Define
  B₀ := {(h, f) ∈ K^7 × K^256 | (S1), (S2), (S3_u) ∀ u ∈ queries}.
Lemma bat_ker_own_le : bat(ker own) ⊆ B₀ — STRUCTURAL given GoodCircle
(CONDITIONAL only through z₀ ≠ z₁).  On the unbalanced model, (S2) reads
coeff 0 + coeff 4 = quarter·ia instead, which is why Z8 counted 264 − 24 = 240
K-values = 1,920 limbs: exact agreement with "batched pivots 1,920".

Independence of the 24 relations (needed for dim B₀, which the constructive
side of (B) parametrises): the (S3_u) involve only f, through evaluation of
the final code at 22 distinct points, and are independent on the final message

§2.3.4  Fresh generators (STRUCTURAL given α_j ≠ 0).  Call u fresh at j if
û_{j+1} ≡ 0, i.e. (1−α_j)·û_j(0,x) + α_j·û_j(1,x) = 0 for every suffix x.
Then R_{ℓ,k}(u) = 0 for all k > j (later rounds read only folds of û_{j+1}),
the lane's terminal and its claim at α are 0, and
  R_{ℓ,j}(u) = ((α_j − X)/α_j) · Σ_{β'} S_{j,0,β'}(u) · Φ(w_j X + c_j + β').
For G (K₄-valued) the S-values S_{j,0,·} are arbitrary in K₄^{N_j}.  For an
F-lane they range over Λ_j ∩ ρ_j·Λ_j with ρ_j = −α_j/(1−α_j) (û_j(1,x) must be
−((1−α_j)/α_j)·û_j(0,x) and both lie in Λ_j).  This is all of K₄ when
Λ_j = K₄, and can be 0 when Λ_j = F and α_j ∉ F.  By P1/P3 the fresh round-j
images, as product degrees of (X − α_j)·q, are:
  G:            [max(1, 28 − N_j), 27]
  mask lane e:  [max(1, e + 2 − N_j), e + 1]   (K₄-linear when Λ_j = K₄)

§2.5.3  B-lo: the low fibres from G (G-low).  Two structural facts and one
conditional one.
  T₀ (STRUCTURAL given z_i.x ≠ 0, z₀ ≠ z₁).  On {c : ood(c) = 0}, the low part
     Q(c)_{<56} depends only on c_{<56}, via a linear T₀ : K^56 → K^56 that is
     bijective and K₄-rational.  (Well-defined: two extensions differ by a
     D-high-type vector with zero OOD, whose quotient has zero low part by
     §2.5.1.  Injective: Q(c) ≡ 0 mod x^28 ⇒ c = L·Q(c) ≡ 0 mod x^28.  Every
     low part extends with zero OOD because x^28·{1, y} evaluated at z₀, z₁
     is invertible when z_i.x ≠ 0 and z₀.y ≠ z₁.y.)
  Fold-onto (STRUCTURAL given K-deg).  f ↦ (Σ_s α_f^s v(4d+s))_{d<14} maps
     K₄^56 onto K^14.
  G-low (CONDITIONAL; same nature as OT_G).  The rows π.symm k, k < 56, are
     copy-inactive (D13Order).  Statement: restriction to these 56 rows,
     ker R_G ∩ ker own⁺_G → K₄^56, is onto (or the weaker form with
     R_G^{-1}(V_{≠G}) of §2.4.4).  Margins: G's F-dimension 4,096 against
     rank R_G + rank own⁺_G ≈ 760 + 380 and 224 prescribed limbs.
Then for every target low ∈ K^14 there is g ∈ G-words ∩ ker R ∩ ker own with
f_{<14}(bat g) = low: G's batched contribution is γ^27·Q(c_G), so choose the
K₄-valued low rows c_low with Fold(T₀ c_low) = γ^{-27}·low (possible by
Fold-onto and T₀ bijective) and complete them by G-low.  Only K₄-values are
available in a G word, which is why K-deg (α_f ∉ K₄), not γ ∉ K₄, is the
operative condition.  Mask lanes can serve instead
(γ^{16+c}·F^56, ten lanes); G is the cleanest.

§2.5.4  Assembly of (B) (STRUCTURAL glue).  Given b ∈ B₀: pick g (G-low) with
low(bat g) = low(b); bat g ∈ B₀ since g ∈ ker own (§2.2.4), so

§2.6  Where the row basis meets the coefficient basis through π

Row-indexed (u_ℓ(r)): the rounds R (prefix folds, eqPrefix, bit classes), the
point claims (eqWeight(openingPoints α j)(r)), σ_ℓ (copyInactiveRows), and
the balancing itself (row 0).  Coefficient-indexed (c_ℓ(k) = u_ℓ(π.symm k)):
extra (libraWeight on k < 56), OOD (evalMessage), opened (exactInitialEncoder
at childIndex q s), and the whole batched block (Q, foldMessage,
roundPolynomial).  The batched weights w = openingWeights are row weights
pushed through π (coeffWeight π), and (S2) is exactly the statement that this
push-forward pairs the quotient back to the row-side claims (image_pairing);
it holds for every π.  Facts about π used, all from D13Order h.transport:
  (π1) k < 89 ⇒ π.symm k ∈ copyInactiveRows.  Used for: D-low cells are
       inactive rows, so a D-low cell is realised in the balanced space as
       e_{π.symm d} − e_{π.symm 56} (both inactive; the second is D-high,
       round-free) — this is how pureRound_libra's cells become balanced
       sources without touching row 0's coefficient; G-low's 56 rows are
       inactive, so σ_G couples them to the other inactive rows; C-Dhigh's
       σ column (coefficient 56).
  (π2) π.symm monotone on k ≥ 89 and π 1023 = 1023: not used by any lemma
       below; it would matter only for a structural witness of OT-row/C-Dhigh
       that needs active rows with known coefficients (§2.4.2).
Where the two bases meet non-structurally: (a) claims and σ restricted to
coefficient-structured absorbers (D-high in C-Dhigh and B_hi; G's low rows in
G-low) — conditional in α, witness via Boolean α plus a π/registry fact;
(b) rowspace(R_ℓ) against OOD/opened in OT for mask lanes and G — OOD is
conditional in z with a clean count, opened is OPEN.  Everything else is on
one side: RC is purely row-side (D's cells enter through their coefficient
indices but pureRound_libra already resolved them), the x^28 law, T₀,
Fold-onto and the B_hi fold part are purely coefficient-side.
The concrete permutation is the Rust ROW_TO_COEFFICIENT (z5::transport()),
a refinement obligation; the Lean theorem takes D13Order as a premise.

§2.7  Alternatives considered

A1 (dual / "no hidden leakage").  Chain0₄ ⊆ R(ker O) iff every round
functional φ with φ∘R ∈ rowspace(O) vanishes on Chain0₄; with §2.2.4 the
expected left kernel of [R; O] is spanned by the nine chain equalities, the
initial ↔ maskSum and terminal ↔ claimMask couplings, and the 24 verifier
relations — the cleanest statement for a numeric gate and for exposition
("the observations reveal no round functional beyond what the verifier's
own checks fix").  As a proof it needs the same rank content; not easier.
A2 (one Schwartz–Zippel over all challenges).  rank[R; O] ≥ 4,872 (Lean
model: 1,084 + 3,788) as non-vanishing of one minor.  The degree bound is
fine (§4), but a witness requires certifying a ≈ 4,900-dimensional minor at
a concrete point; under AGENTS rules 2–3 this is not a kernel computation we
may run, and a compact certificate (LU factors) is ~10^7 field entries.
Rejected as the main route; acceptable only as a last resort for the small
conditional minors (PR6, PR7: 8×8).
A3 (modular (P)/(Q)).  Refuted by Z8b; §2.5.1 explains why (x^28 law) and
§2.5.3 repairs it with one extra absorber (G-low).
A4 (late generators / downward filtration).  Using generators whose rounds
before j vanish (moment conditions on suffix class sums) avoids the ρ_j
issue of fresh F-lane generators, but at rounds ≥ 5 G has no late
generators (27 moment conditions against 2N_j ≤ 30 classes) and lanes with
e ≥ 7 have none at round 7, so the downward route fails exactly where the
upward one succeeds; a mixed route might remove H_Λ (§6).
A5 (per-lane OT in full).  Replaced by OT′ (§2.4.4): proving the lanes in
the order D, G, mask lanes lets each mask lane use the others' own-silent
round images, roughly halving the transversality burden.
A6 (protocol change: a round-free absorber block per lane, as D's
coefficients 56–1023).  It would make OT structural, but the proved
protocol is the specification (v8 is R0); not pursued.
A7 (predicates).  Good₂ (span of 1, α₀..α₅) gives Λ₆ = Λ₇ = K₄ but not the
Λ₄, Λ₅ that fresh F-lane generators need; Good₂′ (∃ i < 7, α_i ∉ K₂) gives
only a two-dimensional Λ₇ and fresh round-6 F-lane generators may vanish
(Λ₆ ∩ ρ₆Λ₆ can be 0); Good₂″ (i < 6) fixes rounds 6–7 by rotation but not
rounds 4–5.  §4 proposes Good₃ (three of α₀..α₃ outside K₂), which implies
H_Λ, with a clean density.

§6  Open points and honest unknowns
-----------------------------------
O1 (main risk) OT-open.  Transversality of the 88 opened-symbol functionals
   of a mask lane or G against its round kernel (or, in the OT′ form, against
   R_ℓ^{-1}(V_{≠ℓ})).  For a fixed query set it is a polynomial condition in α,
   but I have no witness valid for every query set, and the Bezout count over
   fibres is too weak (~2^-5 per query).  Candidate routes: (a) a structured
   α (not Boolean — Boolean α collapses Λ_j to F and loses round rank) at which
   rowspace(R_ℓ) has an explicit "staircase" form whose intersection with any
   88 evaluation functionals of distinct fibres can be shown trivial using the
   circle code's interpolation property (any ≤ 1,023 evaluations independent);
   (b) a dual-distance bound for the punctured code Enc(c(ker R_ℓ)); (c) if
   neither works, the honest fallback is to carry OT-open as an explicit
   hypothesis of the privacy theorem, supported by Z8/Z8c on random vectors —
   that is a numerically checked premise, not a proof, and must be labelled
   so in the release ledger.
O2 Witnesses for GoodGeneric (PR6, PR7, OT-ood is already clean, OT-row,
   C-Dhigh row part, G-low, B_hi relation part).  PR6/PR7 are 8×8 systems with
   integer tail data and should be settled by a short exact computation; the
   others are large minors and share O1's difficulty except that their
   variables are continuous (α, κ, τ), so a single structured witness point
   suffices.  None is in hand.
O3 Model gap F4 (balancing).  1,076 is so far an upper bound for the Lean
   mask space under WideExact batching.  Z8c (1) decides it.
O4 Z8b residual.  My structural count gives 1,804 / 1,996 where Z8b has
   1,812 / 2,000.  The residual is in the direction of more rank, so it cannot
   hide a failure of (B); but if it means one of (S1)/(S2) is not an identity
   for the probe's prover::round_polynomial, the Rust/Lean relation conventions
   differ and R-H must know (cf. the κ⁴·libraWeight mismatch found by Z8).
   Z8c (5) prints the left kernel.
O5 Predicate.  The route needs Λ₄ = K₄; Good₃ supplies it with density
   ≈ 2^-121.  Whether rounds 4–6 can be done without H_Λ (so that the much
   cheaper Good₂′/Good₂″ suffices) is open; A4's mixed upward/downward
   filtration is the candidate.  Whether the STATEMENT holds under Good₂′ with
   only α₆ outside K₂ is untested (Z8c (L)).
O6 Instance facts the final theorem assumes rather than proves: PackField for
   the concrete pack basis (i² = −1, u² = 2 + i); K4 B = image of QM31Exact in
   WideExact (for "ch.sem ∈ K₄ with mass 0"); [K : K₄] = 2; card F = P;
   D13Order for ROW_TO_COEFFICIENT; that the honest trace's semantic lanes are
   F-valued (hw).  Each is a refinement obligation, cheap but not free.
O7 Degree bounds in §4.2 are crude and should be recomputed once the minors
   are fixed; the sum (≈ 2^-104.3, OT-open excluded) has only ~4 bits of slack
   against 2^-100, almost all from OT-row/OT-ood.
O8 Scope.  This design covers clause (i′) of MaskImageOn only.  Clause (ii′)
   (cTilde w − cTilde w' ∈ range A, with the semantic and H1 lanes) needs its
   own design; Z8's (ii′) 25/25 is its numeric support.  The R1 payload (two
   folds, 64 final values, D18 transport) changes the verifier relations
   (two FRI layers) and the low-fibre count of the x^28 law; the structure
   carries over but every number must be redone (Z9).
O9 Efficiency (ZF2).  The reduction and RC are constructive; the conditional
   rank steps produce preimages only through linear-algebra existence
   (Classical.choice).  An efficient simulator needs explicit solving of the
   same systems — feasible (they are linear) but not provided by this proof.
