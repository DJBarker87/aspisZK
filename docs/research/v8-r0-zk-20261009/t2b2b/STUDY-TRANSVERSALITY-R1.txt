§1  The two premises as events over the R1 challenge draw
--------------------------------------------------------
§1.1  R1 objects (from verifier.rs; R1 = Variant with folds() = 2, final_len 64).

  Lanes.  29 committed lanes l = 0..28 in two leaves per child: C1 leaf =
  26 M31 symbols (semantic lanes 0..15, mask-only lanes 16..25 = m0..m9) plus
  one QM31 symbol (lane 28 = D, offset 104); C2 leaf = two QM31 symbols
  (lanes 26 = H1 padding, 27 = G).  Each lane is a 1,024-row word whose
  natural coefficients are the rows permuted by the transport (D18 in R1).
  Code.  Message dimension 1,024, codeword on 2^20 circle points grouped in
  2^18 = 262,144 fibres of 4 children (domain::FibreIndex < 1<<18);
  Merkle trees of arity 8, depth 6 over fibres.  Fold 1 (circle fold, α_0)
  gives a 256-coefficient message on 2^18 line points, committed
  (folded_root) in 2^16 leaves of 4 values; fold 2 (line fold, α_1) gives the
  64-value final message.
  Challenge rows (Transcript): rows 0..26 semantic prefix (including the
  α-row 15..24 of the Libra/sumcheck part, circle points z_0, z_1), row 27 γ,
  28 κ, 29 α_0, 30 α_1, 31 the 22 queries, 32 ρ (final-weight batching):
  33 FS rows.  γ, κ, α_0, α_1 ∈ E = WideExact (K in the design notation);
  semantic challenges and ρ ∈ QM31 = K₄.  The 22 queries are distinct fibres
  u_0..u_21 < 2^18 (resume() rejects repeats); their second-layer leaves are
  u_i/4 (two queries may share a second-layer leaf).

  What the verifier sees, per lane l (the "own" observations own_l):
    claims[0..2][l]   three MLE claims at the opening points (row basis)
    extra[l]          the Libra functional (coefficient basis, k < 56 in R0)
    before_z1[l], y1[l]   the two OOD values (coefficient basis)
    opened_l(u_i, s)  the codeword symbol at child s of each queried fibre:
                      22 × 4 = 88 symbols (F-valued for lanes 0..25, K₄ for
                      26..28)
  and batched across lanes (bat, through γ):
    inactive          γ-batched inactive-row sum (0 on balanced mask tapes)
    round0, round1    7 + 7 E-coefficients of the two FRI round polynomials
    folded values     4 E-values at each second-layer leaf u_i/4; one of them
                      is the fold-1 value at u_i (checked: folded == values[u%4])
                      and that one is a function of own_l (the opened symbols
                      and the OOD interpolant), so on own-silent tapes it is 0
    final message     64 E-values
  plus the Merkle roots (hash commitments; their hiding is a separate,
  salted-leaf ROM statement and plays no role in the rank premises).

  Counts on the R1 payload (cf. R0's 1,872 own / 1,912 bat limbs):
    own per F-lane  12 (claims) + 4 (extra) + 8 (OOD) + 88 (opened) = 112 limbs
                    (matches the R1 gate's "eligible C1 raw ranks: 112");
    own per K₄-lane 4·(3 + 1 + 2 + 88) = 376 limbs;
    bat             8·(14 + 64 + 3·q₂) limbs before the verifier relations,
                    q₂ = number of distinct second-layer leaves (22 unless two
                    queries share u/4).  The verifier relations on own-silent
                    tapes are, by the R0 pattern (design §2.2.4) extended to two
                    layers: round_boundary and round_eval for each of the two
                    rounds (4 E-relations), fold-2 consistency at each queried
                    second-layer point (q₂), fold-1 = folded[u%4] (already used
                    to remove one value per query), and the final ρ-batched
                    check (subsumed by the q₂ point checks).  This count is a
                    prediction for Z9; it has not been measured on R1.

§1.2  The premises as events.  Fix all challenges except the queries and
write Q = {u_0, ..., u_21} for the query set and X(Q) for its 88 children.
Notation as DESIGN-T2b-2b §0: for lane l, W_l its balanced word space,
R_l : W_l → rounds its round map (depends on the α-row only), own⁺_l = (own_l,
σ_l); Rel_l the round-determined relations ({claim_0} for e ≤ 13; {claim_0,
claim_2} for G and e ≥ 14; {extra} for D).

  OT_l(ch, Q):  own⁺_l(ker R_l) = own⁺_l(W_l) ∩ ker Rel_l.
     Equivalent dual form (used below): let
       V_l(ch) := rowspace(R_l) + span(claims, extra, OOD, σ_l)   (query-free)
     a space of F-functionals on W_l of F-dimension r_l ≈ 520–760 (R0 counts;
     R1 identical, since the sumcheck and row layout are the same and only the
     transport differs).  Then OT_l fails iff some nonzero functional in
     span{ev_x : x ∈ X(Q)} lies in V_l(ch) beyond the identities Rel_l, i.e.
       FAIL_OT_l(ch, Q) :⇔ span_F{ev_x|_{W_l} : x ∈ X(Q)} ∩ V_l(ch) ≠ 0
                         (modulo Rel_l).
     OT-open is ⋀_{l ∈ {m0..m9, G}} OT_l, together with C-Dhigh's query part
     for D (TP_D).

  BCond(ch, Q):  bat(ker own ∩ ker R) = bat(ker own).  On R1 (§1.1a) this is
     V_G = B₀: G's own-silent round kernel must reach all 952 limbs of B₀.  bat
     carries the second-layer opened values (evaluations of the folded batched
     word at the 66 other children of the leaves u_i/4) and the C_u/L_u
     relations tie them to the first layer, so V_G = B₀ is again a (TRANS)
     statement: G's 352 opened limbs (and its claims, Libra, OOD and σ) must be
     transversal, inside ker R_G, to the 952 batched functionals — the R1 form
     of Gb with V of size rank R_G (660) + own (376) + 952.  For comparison, the
     R0 reduction (Dprime §2.6) went through query-free conditions (GoodWrap,
     NZ, K-deg, Ga, G2) plus two query-dependent ones of the same dual form:
       TP_D:  the 12 K₄-functionals Φ_D (claims_0..2(Lq), ind(Lq), the 8 free
              h-dims) stay independent modulo the 22-node evaluation block;
              FAIL_TP_D(ch, Q) :⇔ span{ev at X(Q)} ∩ (Φ_D + ...) ≠ 0 on the
              D-high block;
       Gb:    E_G (2 OOD + 88 opened) has full rank on
              G ∩ ker R ∩ ker Rw ∩ ker(T∘bat) — i.e. OT_G with 104 more
              query-free functionals added to V_G.

  On R1 TP_D is not needed (V_D plays no role once V_G = B₀); Gb_R1 is the
  whole of B-open.
  The premise family is therefore one shape repeated 12–13 times:
     (TRANS)  For a query-free functional space V(ch) of dimension r on a
              message space M of dimension n (n = 1,024 per K₄/F-coordinate),
              the 88 evaluation functionals at X(Q) are independent modulo V.
  with (r, n) ≈ (544, 1,024) for a mask lane of exponent 21, ≈ (780, 1,024)
  per K₄-limb-block for G, and r ≈ 12 K₄-functionals on a structured
  coefficient block for TP_D.

§1.3  What has to be shown.  A ledger term ε_T with
     Pr_{ch}[ ⋁_l FAIL_OT_l ∨ FAIL_TP_D ∨ FAIL_Gb ] ≤ ε_T,
  the probability over the full FS draw (rows 0..32; the queries of row 31 are
  drawn after every challenge that V depends on, so V(ch) is fixed when Q is
  drawn — the order is favourable).  Target: ε_T small enough to sit beside the
  ~2^-104.3 privacy terms, i.e. ε_T ≤ 2^-106 or so.  Two shapes of argument
  are possible:
   (P-SZ)  for EVERY query set Q, the failure locus is contained in the zero
           set of a nonzero polynomial in the continuous challenges (α-row,
           z, γ, κ, α_f) of degree D_Q; then ε_T ≤ max_Q D_Q·(1+δ)/|K₄| per
           premise.  Needs a witness challenge for every Q (2^18 choose 22
           ≈ 2^306 sets), or a structural argument covering all Q at once.
   (P-CNT) for fixed ch, a bound on the fraction of query sets that fail,
           uniformly over ch (or off a small bad set of ch).
  Neither exists today; 35 challenge vectors were checked (Z8c/Z8e), each with
  one query set.

§2  Route P — proving a failure-mass bound
-----------------------------------------
§2.1  The one fact that every argument uses (PROVED here; standard).  Let M
be the lane message space (natural basis, 1,024 coordinates) and ev_x the
evaluation functional at a domain point x.  Write the message by channels
m = 4d + s (s = 0..3, d < 256); evaluation at the four children of a fibre is
equivalent to evaluating all four channels at that fibre's line node t_u
(fibre_inverse; x_u, y_u ≠ 0).  Then:
  (E1) [interpolation]  For distinct nodes t_1..t_k and any block
       B = {c + e : e < k} of channel coordinates with c = 0, or c a power of
       two ≥ k with T_c(t_i) ≠ 0 (NZ), the k node evaluations are bijective
       on B (N′_{c+e} = N′_c·N′_e; Vandermonde in the N′_e, e < k).
  (E2) [high support]  A family Φ of functionals whose natural-coordinate
       vectors are zero on the channel coordinates d < 22 keeps its rank on
       {channels vanishing at 22 distinct nodes}: the coordinates d ∈
       [22, 256) are bijective there (kernel = polynomials of degree < 22
       with 22 zeros).
  (E3) [rank-one restriction]  On a block N′_c·P_{<k} (and on the whole
       channel), ev_t is a rank-one Hankel functional: H_{ev_t}(a, b) =
       ev_t(N′_c·t^{a+b}) = N′_c(t)·t^a·t^b.  Hence a combination of j
       evaluations has Hankel rank ≤ j on every A×B with A·B inside the block.
Every query-dependent premise is an instance of (TRANS) (§1.2): a
query-free space V must meet span{ev_x : x ∈ X(Q)} only in 0.  (E1)–(E2)
settle (TRANS) when V vanishes on an interpolation block or is supported on
high coordinates; (E3) gives a query-free sufficient condition when V is
small.  None of the three applies to the actual premises, for the reasons
below.

§2.2  Why the two obvious formulations do not give a bound.
  (P-SZ, per query set).  For fixed Q, FAIL_OT_l(·, Q) is the vanishing of
  all maximal minors of [V(ch); E(Q)], polynomials in the α-row (and z) of
  degree ≤ 10 per row of claims and ≤ e+1 per round row through β powers —
  rough total degree D_Q ≤ 10·r ≈ 4·10^3 for r ≈ 400.  Schwartz–Zippel then
  gives Pr_ch[FAIL | Q] ≤ D_Q/|K₄| ≈ 2^-112 PROVIDED the minor is not
  identically zero for this Q, which needs a witness ch*(Q).  A witness is a
  challenge at which the lane's round rowspace is explicit; the only explicit
  points are Boolean or base-field α, and there Λ_j collapses to F (D20.1),
  rank R_l drops (Z7 (B), Z8c structure B/L), and the minor is zero for
  trivial reasons.  So no single structured witness works, and an
  X-dependent witness would have to be exhibited for ~C(2^18, 22) ≈ 2^306
  query sets.  This is the D23.2 obstruction: the functionals of V that do
  not depend on α at all — σ (the inactive-row indicator, 810 of 1,024
  rows, dense in natural coordinates) and the OOD evaluations — cannot be
  moved by any witness, and σ is not supported on high coordinates, so (E2)
  is unavailable even at a degenerate α.
  (P-CNT, per challenge).  For fixed ch, count bad fibres sequentially: the
  k-th fibre is bad iff its four evaluations are dependent on
  V + span(previous), i.e. iff the fibre is a common zero of a nonzero
  subspace of messages; one nonzero message has ≤ 1,023 zeros on the circle,
  so Pr[bad k-th fibre] ≤ 4·1,023/(2^18 − k) ≈ 2^-6, and 22 fibres give
  ≈ 2^-1.5.  This is the "Bezout ~2^13 bad fibres" count of DESIGN-T2b-2b
  §2.4.3: correct, but four to five orders of magnitude in the exponent too
  weak.  It uses one witness message per step; a stronger count would need
  the common zero set of a whole (n − r − k)-dimensional message space, i.e.
  structure of V that is not available.
  (Heuristic only.)  Dimension count: the bad locus {Q : (TRANS) fails} is
  cut out on the 22-parameter family of query sets by a rank drop of
  expected codimension n − r − 88 + 1 (≥ 392 for the largest mask lane on
  R1-sized data, ≥ 475 for the G-factor shape, ≥ 860 for D), so for
  "generic" V it is empty.  This explains why every numerical check passes,
  but it is not a proof: V is highly structured (eq-MLE tensors, Boolean-face
  σ, the natural-basis tensor structure of rows ≥ 479 noted in Dprime R5),
  and structure is exactly what can make a high-codimension locus non-empty.

§2.3  Candidate witnesses and where each stops.
  (W1) Boolean / base-field α.  Kills Λ_j; V loses rank; the minor vanishes.
       Dead (Z8c structures B, L; D21.3).
  (W2) α with the round rowspace in "staircase" form (DESIGN-T2b-2b §6 O1(a)).
       Would need R_l's rowspace, pushed through π, to be supported on
       natural coordinates ≥ 88 modulo explicit pieces, so that (E2) applies;
       the round rows read every row with weight eqPrefix(α, r_<j) ≠ 0 and π
       spreads them over all coordinates; σ and the claims are dense for every
       α.  No candidate found.
  (W3) Query-free Hankel / secant condition (Dprime §5 R2 made concrete by
       (E3)).  Sufficient: every nonzero φ ∈ V has Hankel rank ≥ 89 on some
       A×B ⊆ M.  Certifiable by ONE nonzero minor (hence a Schwartz–Zippel
       bound with ONE query-free witness) only through the Kronecker
       stacking argument: if the m generators φ_1..φ_m of V satisfy
       rank[H_{φ_1}J | … | H_{φ_m}J] = 89·m for a column set J of size 89,
       no nonzero combination has Hankel rank ≤ 88.  This needs
       89·m ≤ (number of rows of H) ≤ ~510 on a 1,024-dimensional message
       space, i.e. m ≤ 5.  The OT-open spaces have m ≈ 400–660 (mask lanes,
       G) and Gb adds 104: impossible by a factor of ~100.  TP_D's residual
       has 12 K₄-functionals on two 256-coordinate B-channels at 22 nodes:
       12·23 = 276 > 128 rows of a channel Hankel: also impossible.  (W3)
       works only for spaces of ≤ 4–5 functionals — which is what Route M
       produces (§3.2).  On R1 the B-open form V_G = B₀ (§1.1a) is worse, not
       better, for (W3): G must absorb 952 batched limbs plus its own 376
       inside ker R_G (rank 660).
  (W4) Dual distance of the punctured code Enc(c(ker R_l)) (O1(b)).  The
       dual of a subcode contains dual words of support ≤ 88 exactly when
       (TRANS) fails; a dual-distance bound is a restatement, not a route.
Conclusion of §2.3: for the premises as they stand (large V), I found no
formulation that yields a failure-mass bound, and the structural reason is
general — a dense, challenge-independent functional (σ, OOD) together with a
challenge-dependent space of dimension ~400–660 cannot be separated from
evaluations at adversarially placed points by any of the available tools
(interpolation, high support, Hankel rank).

§2.5  Feasibility estimate (Route P).  For a proved bound on OT-open and
B-open in their current form: low.  I would put it below 15% within a few
weeks of focused work, because the three known tools provably cannot reach
spaces of this size (§2.3 W3 is a hard counting limit, not a missing idea),
and a new tool would have to exploit the specific tensor structure of the
eq-MLE rows against the doubling-curve structure of the evaluations — a
research problem of its own (Dprime R5 is the only lead, and it needs the
transport-identity premise on rows ≥ 479 in Lean as well).  What Route P can
realistically deliver is (a) a Schwartz–Zippel bound for the small,
query-free pieces (Ga, G2, GoodWrap, PR6/PR7, C_ll) once exact witnesses are
certified, and (b) an (E3)/Kronecker certificate for any premise that can be
reduced to ≤ 4–5 functionals — which the current premises cannot.

§3  Route M — mask redesign (D23.5)
----------------------------------
§3.0  What has to change, in one sentence.  Each premise asks a mask block
to absorb the 88 queried evaluations while staying silent on a large
query-free space V that it shares with the rounds, the claims and σ.  The
premises become structural exactly when the absorbing block is a block that V
does not read (E1), or when the absorbed observations are functions of one
uniformly random vector that V does not touch at all.  Inside a sumcheck lane
no such block exists: every row enters every round through eqPrefix(α, ·) ≠ 0
and every claim through eq(pt, ·) ≠ 0 (DESIGN-T2b-2b §2.4.3).  So Route M
must either add randomness that the sumcheck and the claims do not see, or
stop relying on the sumcheck lanes for the absorption.  Three concrete
changes follow; M1 and M2 together remove both premises from the theorem, M3
removes them without touching the verifier.

Cost method used throughout (ESTIMATE).  The only measured marginal unit is
the cost of one query pair moved between transactions: 49,524–49,562 CU
(SPLIT10-COSTS-20261015.md, five fixtures).  Its composition (P2-CU-
RECONCILIATION.md, older pairing stage): Merkle ≈ 9.1k CU/query (≈ 19 digest
calls over 160–512-byte inputs), parse ≈ 1.2k CU/query (strict scan of
736 query bytes), the rest (≈ 39k) is four_dots + the two folds + the
64-weight ρ accumulation.  In four_dots one F-lane symbol costs 8 limb
products per child and one QM31 symbol 32 limb products (EbyKDot), so an
extra QM31 lane adds ≈ 32/(26·8 + 3·32) ≈ 10% of the dots work.  I assign
dots ≈ 8–15k CU/query (the folds and accumulation do not depend on the lane
count).  Unit E×K products are taken as 120–250 CU (lazy-reduced EbyKDot
push) and K×K as 60–100 CU.  Budgets: stage1 (tx1) ≈ 1.343M CU (target
1.3M, cap 1.4M; 57k under the cap); worst stage2 settlement (withdrawal,
255 pairs) 1,368,461 CU, 31,539 under the cap.  Every CU figure below is an
estimate to ± a factor of ~1.5 and must be measured before adoption.

§3.1  M1 — a γ-batched FRI mask lane Z (removes B-open).  PROPOSED.
  (Revised for the R1 opening of §1.1a: no chord quotient, OOD as weight
  kinds 5–6.  An earlier draft sent one combined value μ_Z = ⟨Z, w⟩ after κ;
  that is UNSOUND — a value chosen after γ and κ can cancel the error of
  false claims of the other lanes — and is withdrawn.)
  Change (prover).  Sample Z uniformly in E^1024 (natural coefficients) and
  commit its codeword in the C2 leaf next to lanes 26/27 (one E symbol, or two
  QM31 symbols, per child), i.e. before γ.  Z is a 30th PCS lane that is not a
  sumcheck lane: it has no round contribution, no balancing and no semantic
  use.  Its seven pairing values v_{Z,k} := ⟨Z, W_k⟩ (k = 0 inactive, 1–3 the
  eq-MLE points, 4 Libra, 5–6 OOD) are sent with the other lanes' values in
  the prefix (before γ), exactly as for lanes 0..28.  Batched word:
       q_tot := Σ_{l ≤ 28} γ^l·c_l + γ^29·Z,
  and every parts[k] of prepared_claim gains the term γ^29·v_{Z,k}.
  Change (verifier).  Leaf parse/hash +32 bytes per child; four_dots adds
  γ^29·Z(child); prepared_claim's six dot products run over 30 lanes; the
  transcript absorbs 7 more E values (or 6 if Z is excluded from kind 0).
  Nothing else: FRI degree bound (1,024), folds, final length 64, 22 queries,
  the 48 relations, unchanged.
  Why B-open disappears (PROVED here; uses only γ ≠ 0 and that every batched
  observation of R1 — round0, round1, the folded-word values, final — is a
  function of q_tot).  Let x be an own-silent tape (all lanes including Z:
  opened = 0, v_{·,k} = 0).  Then q_tot(x) vanishes at X(Q) (each term is
  γ^l·opened_l = 0) and ⟨q_tot(x), W_k⟩ = Σ_l γ^l·v_{l,k}(x) = 0 for every
  kind k.  Let y be the tape with every sumcheck lane 0 and Z-part
  γ^-29·q_tot(x).  Then y has no round contribution, own_l(y) = 0 for l ≤ 28,
  opened_Z(y) = γ^-29·q_tot(x)|X = 0, v_{Z,k}(y) = γ^-29⟨q_tot(x), W_k⟩ = 0,
  inactiveSum and maskSum of y are 0, and q_tot(y) = q_tot(x), so
  bat(y) = bat(x).  Hence bat(ker own ∩ ker R) = bat(ker own) for every
  challenge and every query set; on R1 this replaces V_G = B₀ outright (Z
  alone covers B₀, structurally).  Distributionally: given public data,
  q_tot is uniform on {q : q|X(Q) = recomputed values, ⟨q, W_k⟩ = parts[k]},
  so the whole FRI part is simulated from public data — the standard
  "random full-degree polynomial in the FRI batch" argument.  E-valued (not
  QM31) is required: γ^29·K₄^1024 is not an E-subspace; two QM31 lanes at
  γ^29, γ^30 are equivalent off γ ∈ K₄ (row privacy_gamma_in_subfield_event_only,
  2^-124).
  What it also removes.  BCond_R1 / Gb_R1, the Dprime apparatus (DHighChar,
  GCover, TP_D, G1–G3, Ga/Gb, GoodWrap, lowShadow, etaRel, C_ll) and J7′/J7a,
  and the dual-pad batched correction of PRIVACY-RANK-PATH.md: Z :=
  −γ^-29·Σ_c γ^c·r_c has zero opened values and zero v_{Z,k} for residuals r_c
  with zero raw observations.  It does NOT remove OT-open (Z has no rounds).
  Proof bytes.  +32 B × 4 children × 22 queries = +2,816 B, plus 7 × 32 B of
  pairing values: +3,040 B (+2.5% of the 122,231-byte proof; staged, outside
  the counted transactions).
  Verifier CU (ESTIMATE).  Per query: leaf hash +128 B (+64–130 CU), parse of
  4 more E values (+150–250 CU), four_dots +64 limb products per child
  (≈ +10–20% of the dots work: +0.8–2.5k CU) → +1.0–2.9k CU/query, i.e.

§3.2  M2 — Libra-complete D with an evaluation reservoir (removes OT-open).
  PROPOSED.
  Change (prover).  D (lane 28) becomes a full Libra mask: its natural
  coefficients 28·j + e (j = 0..9, e ≤ 27), i.e. coefficients 0..279, carry

  positions instead of 4), with power tables for all ten α_j.  The sumcheck
  degree bound (27 per round) and the masked-terminal formula (extra[28] is

  Why OT-open disappears.  (Q′) needs, for every chain vector t with terminal
  0, an own-silent tape whose rounds are t.  (a) Libra cover (STRUCTURAL; the
  pureRound_libra/D_absorb pattern of DESIGN-T2b-2b §2.3.5 for all rounds):

§3.3  M3 — enforce the premises by honest-prover rejection (removes both
  premises from the privacy statement; verifier unchanged).  PROPOSED.
  Change (prover only).  After deriving the challenges of an attempt, the
  honest prover evaluates P(ch) := Good₃ ∧ OT ∧ BCond_R1 — and, for clause (ii′),
  C1T-open/H1T-open and the opened part of C1Lift — (exact linear algebra
  on public data: the challenges, the query set, the transport) and, if it
  fails, discards the attempt and starts a fresh one with new masks, salts
  and attempt secrets (the StateOnlyAttemptSecrets / durable nonce
  reservation path already exists, PRIVACY-RANK-PATH.md "Optional honest-
  prover rank rejection").  Nothing about an aborted attempt is published.
  Why this is enough.  P depends only on ch; in the ROM, ch of a fresh
  attempt is uniform and independent of the commitments, so acceptance is
  independent of the witness and of the masks.  Accepted transcripts have
  ch ~ Uniform | P and view | ch witness-independent by the already-planned
  HVZK_on P; the simulator rejection-samples ch | P from public data and
  programs the oracle.  The privacy error then contains no badMass(P) term
  at all — not for the transversality premises and not for Good₃ either —
  only the abort-aware oracle-programming terms (expected attempts ×
  hit probability of an aborted attempt's hidden oracle points).
  Costs.  Verifier CU 0, proof bytes 0, soundness rows unchanged (the
  verifier does not know about P).  Prover: one P check per attempt, of
  Z8c/Z8e size (≈ 12 lane rank tests of ~500×1,024 F and 4·(~800×1,024) K₄
  dimensions plus the 1,912-dimensional batched check; the Z8c jobs ran in
  ≈ 314 MB, the R1 rank gate in ≈ 16.5 s including compilation): seconds per
  attempt on a desktop; expected attempts 1/(1 − Pr[¬P]), numerically 1.
  Completeness/liveness only depends on Pr[¬P] being small, which needs no
  proof for privacy (a cap on attempts with an explicit failure exit is
  enough; Z8c/Z8e/Z10 have never seen a failure).
  What it does NOT give.  It does not make the premises true for transcripts
  produced by any other prover (irrelevant for ZK, which is about the honest
  prover), and it moves work into ZF1: the FS composition must be stated for
  a prover with aborts (FSExperiments with a retry loop, fresh coins per
  attempt, the simulator's conditional sampler), cf. the eprint 2023/245
  warning recorded in PRIVACY-RANK-PATH.md.  It adds a refinement obligation:
  the Rust check must decide exactly the Lean predicate P on the exact R1
  payload (the Z9 model), and the honest prover's correctness now includes
  "never publishes a transcript with ¬P".
  Lean.  Proved theorems unchanged: T2a's chain is already stated "on P";
  T2b-2b keeps hBc and the OT premises as hypotheses (the current J9 shape);
  the only new statement is the abort-aware ZK_FS (ZF1 scope, open anyway),
  whose error term drops badMass P.

§3.4  D24.4 (π_R1 and the opened part of C1Lift) in the same comparison.
  D24.4 says that under π_R1 the eligible semantic coefficients of each
  column form the top block of quarter indices i ∈ [208, 256) (classes
  s = 0, 1 exactly that block of 48; classes 2, 3 a subset of it plus sparse
  i ≡ 3 mod 4), so the R0 Vandermonde argument (opened_prefix) does not
  transfer, and names Variant D (D13 transport) or a digit-map search (J11b).
  Observation (STRUCTURAL by (E1); not in the (ii′) design, to be checked by
  the lead against the registry): the top block contains the dyadically
  aligned sub-block i ∈ [224, 246).  Since 224 = 2^5 + 2^6 + 2^7 and every
  e < 32 has bits only in 0..4, N_{224+e} = N_224·N_e for e < 22, so on that
  sub-block the 22-node system is diag(N_224(X_u))·Vandermonde(N_0..N_21) —
  invertible for EVERY set of 22 distinct nodes once N_224(X_u) =
  T_32·T_64·T_128(X_u) ≠ 0, which is the (NZ) circle-order fact already
  recorded as a J7a/J10 obligation (X_u is the x-coordinate of a point of
  order 2^20, and T_{2^k}(x(P)) = x(2^k P) = 0 only for 2^k P of order 4).
  The (ii′) design's "N_192·g, g ∈ span{N_16..N_63}, non-Vandermonde"
  analysis uses the whole block; a 22-element aligned sub-block suffices for
  surjectivity onto the 88 opened values.  Hence:
   - classes s = 0, 1: the opened part of C1Lift is STRUCTURAL on R1 as built
     (no Variant D, no digit-map change), given NZ for k = 5, 6, 7;
   - classes s = 2, 3: structural iff their eligible subset contains some
     aligned block c + [0, 22) with c ≡ 0 (mod 32), c + 21 < 256, inside the
     eligible rows — a finite check of the registry (minutes, no search over
     4!⁵·120 maps); if not, the class needs a different aligned block
     (c ≡ 0 mod 2^m for a block of size 2^m ≥ 22 fits only m = 5) or the
     remaining-limbs premise C1T-open absorbs it.
  Cost comparison if the observation fails for classes 2, 3:
   - Variant D (D13 transport with two folds): restores opened_prefix
     verbatim, but reintroduces the transport component of the verifier's
     weight evaluation that D18 removed by construction (recorded as the
     "3.5M transport component of R-E4's V2" in D18.1; even a 10× better
     implementation is far beyond either transaction's margin). Not viable on
     R1's budget.
   - J11b digit-map search: product-form digit permutations keep D18.1's O(1)
     dual folds, so verifier cost ≈ 0 if a map exists; cost is a probe and a
     re-run of the Z9/Z5 gates.
   - M3 (§3.3) also covers it: C1T-open and the opened part of C1Lift are
     rank conditions on public data, so they join P and are enforced by
     rejection at zero verifier cost.
  None of M1/M2 helps here (they act on the mask lanes, not on the semantic
  lanes' eligible noise).

Recommendation.
  1. D23.5: adopt M3 (honest-prover rejection on the public predicate P),
     and keep M1 as an optional hardening if ~25–70k CU of headroom appears.
     - M3 is the only option that removes the transversality family from the
       privacy statement without touching the verifier, the proof bytes or
       any soundness row.  Every proved theorem (T2a, T2b-1, T2b-2a, ZF-A, J1,
       J3, J5a) and the planned T2b-2b statement (premises as hypotheses of
       P) stay as they are.  The price is paid where work is open anyway: ZF1
       must be stated for a prover with aborts; the honest prover runs the
       exact P check (seconds per attempt); and the Rust check must be shown
       to decide the Lean P on the R1 payload of Z9.  The same mechanism also
       drops Good₃'s mass and C1T-open/H1T-open from the privacy error.
     - M1 is cheap in Lean and proves B-open away in a few lines (on R1 it
       replaces "G alone covers B₀" by "Z alone covers B₀, structurally"); it is
       the standard ZK-FRI mask.  It does nothing for OT-open, and its
       +24–69k CU does not fit stage2's worst margin today.
     - M2 removes OT-open and Good₃'s round-cover condition but costs
       ≈ +55k CU in stage2 (31.5k left) and still leaves one numerical
       certificate.
     - Route P should not be the main line: the available tools have a hard
       capacity limit (Kronecker ≤ 5 functionals against ~400–1,900 in the
       premises; the R1 form V_G = B₀ is the largest), and the dimension
       heuristic that explains the numerics is not a proof.  Z10 (§A) finds no
       failure on random or structured query sets of an R1-sized model, which
       supports M3's completeness assumption (Pr[¬P] ≈ 0) but bounds nothing.
  2. D24.4: before choosing Variant D or J11b, check the aligned sub-block of
     §3.4.  If classes 2 and 3 also contain an aligned 22-block of eligible
     coefficients, the opened part of C1Lift is structural on R1 as built at
     zero cost; if not, J11b (≈ 0 CU) or M3 cover it.  Variant D is not
     affordable on R1.
Trade-off stated plainly.  M3 gives a privacy statement free of the
transversality premises at zero on-chain cost, in exchange for a prover that
may retry, an abort-aware Fiat–Shamir theorem, and the obligation that the
deployed prover really runs the P check.  M1+M2 give a premise-free statement
(up to one certified determinant) for any honest prover with no aborts, at
roughly 65–145k CU and +3 KB that R1 does not currently have.  Proving the
premises as they stand is unlikely to succeed.
