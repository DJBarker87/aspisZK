8. Interpretation (factual)
---------------------------
* Only (B), all alpha in F, loses rank in the constructed silent image, and
  only at round 7: 93 of 112 (limbs 24, 26, 21, 22; u-limb 21 <= 24 as D20.1
  bounds). Rounds 5 and 6 are full (112) under (B). The unconstrained image
  under (B) also loses only at round 7 (103; u-limb 23). The deficit 1,076 -
  1,038 = 38 equals the chain0 excess rank, and every chain0 excess pivot is
  at round 7. The (B) totals and the (i') pivot (7,14,2) coincide with Z4c's
  alpha = all-one control (1,038; pivot (7,14,2)).
* (C), (D), (E) (alpha_0 up to alpha_6 in a 3-dimensional F-subspace W
  containing 1), (F) (alpha_1 in span_F{1, alpha_0}) and (G) (alpha_5..9 in F)
  lose nothing: silent 1,076, unconstrained 1,084, all targets contained.
  In each, Lambda_j = K from j = 2 (C, D, E) or j = 3 (F) on, although
  span_F{1, alpha_0..alpha_4} has dimension 3 in C, D, E.
* (H): restricting the mask-only words to rows with r_<j = 0 leaves the
  round-j projection of the unconstrained image full except at round 7 (108).
  The silent round-j projection drops at rounds 6 (60) and 7 (32). Rounds 8
  and 9 stay full (D). Under this restriction the per-lane row count
  (16 at j = 6, 8 at j = 7) is below the 113 raw observations, so the silent
  (H) matrices at j >= 4 contain no mask-only contribution; the silent (H)
  numbers therefore mix the Lambda_j restriction with loss of silent
  combinations.
* With uniform alpha (A), the pivot sets reproduce D20.1's lane ranges for
  the mask-only lanes at round 5 (lane e covers 0..e+1). G's unconstrained
  pivots are 0-27 at round 5, 12-27 at round 6 and 20-27 at round 7
  (D20.1 states 12-27 at round 5, 19-27 at round 6, 23-27 at round 7).

9. Code
-------
z7.rs is a module of probe.rs, dispatched by `--z7`, following the z5.rs and
z6.rs precedent (they are modules, not separate [[bin]] targets; the probe's
routines are private, so a separate binary could not call them without
editing probe.rs). probe.rs changes only by the `mod z7` declaration and the
`--z7` dispatch line. Called unchanged: Ech, lane_eliminate, compensated_rows,
pure_c, full_c, copied_ech, initial_zero, summarize, pass_count, raw_c,
tail_row, round_row, compute_targets, normalized, normalize_d, final_value,
balance_trace, real::instances. Copied: z6.rs `trial` (adds z and queries to
the return), pure_c's body as pure_h (one added row filter on the mask-only
lanes), last_coeff's chain loop as round_polys (returns all rounds).
