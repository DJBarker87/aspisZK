   terminal, masks, balancing) is the same in R1 and R0 (map sections 3-5). The
   K4-valued chain target is therefore the same space: dim_F Chain0_4 =
   4 * 269 = 1,076, and the initial-retained image has dimension 1,080. The
   silent image lies in Chain0_4 (T2b-2a does not depend on the payload), so
   the silent rank is at most 1,076.

   deficits come from Lambda_7 lying in a proper subfield, which is a fact
   about the semantic layer. Equality needs the R1 analogue of BCond (E4). The
   R1 silence has fewer batched conditions than R0 (1,336 limbs against
   2,112), but the coordinates are different functionals: the folded-word
   values have no R0 counterpart. Containment in R0's span is therefore not an
   argument.

   relations: ia, S2a, S2b, S1, 22 C_u and 22 L_u (map section 6). That is
   384 limbs, so dim B0 = 1,336 - 384 = 952 when the 22 folded fibres u/4
   are distinct.
E4 BCond (R1). bat(ker own ∩ ker R) = bat(ker own) = 952.

   etaRel in R1. Predicted: V_D = 952 - 24 = 928, V_G = 952, and
   B0 = V_D + V_G.
