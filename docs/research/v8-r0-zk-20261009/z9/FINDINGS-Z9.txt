0. Expectations registered before any measurement
-------------------------------------------------
E1 Chain target. The semantic layer (rows 0..26, 10 rounds of K4[X]<=27,
   terminal, masks, balancing) is the same in R1 and R0 (map sections 3-5). The
   K4-valued chain target is therefore the same space: dim_F Chain0_4 =
   4 * 269 = 1,076, and the initial-retained image has dimension 1,080. The
   silent image lies in Chain0_4 (T2b-2a does not depend on the payload), so
   the silent rank is at most 1,076.
E2 Silent rank. Expected 1,076 on A and J, 1,038 on B and 1,068 on I. These
   deficits come from Lambda_7 lying in a proper subfield, which is a fact
   about the semantic layer. Equality needs the R1 analogue of BCond (E4). The
   R1 silence has fewer batched conditions than R0 (1,336 limbs against
   2,112), but the coordinates are different functionals: the folded-word
   values have no R0 counterpart. Containment in R0's span is therefore not an
   argument.
E3 B0. The annihilator of bat(ker own) is spanned by the 48 R1 verifier
   relations: ia, S2a, S2b, S1, 22 C_u and 22 L_u (map section 6). That is
   384 limbs, so dim B0 = 1,336 - 384 = 952 when the 22 folded fibres u/4
   are distinct.
E4 BCond (R1). bat(ker own ∩ ker R) = bat(ker own) = 952.
E5 (i'), (ii'). 25/25 on A and J. On B and I, (i') 0/25 and (ii') 25/25, as in Z8/Z8c.
E6 D23 on R1. R1 has no chord quotient, so the low shadow has no analogue.
   D-high (D coefficients < 56 zero) gives q1_d = 0 for d < 14, and so
   final_0..final_2 = 0 exactly (24 limbs), with final_3 free. The R0
   conjugate relation (Fact 3) needs the whole once-folded message in the
   view, but R1 discloses only 159 of q1's 256 E-functionals, so I expect no
   etaRel in R1. Predicted: V_D = 952 - 24 = 928, V_G = 952, and
   B0 = V_D + V_G.

3. Answers
----------
Q1 (gate, true R1 PCS). MEASURED. The silent rank of the K4 chain target is
   1,076 = dim_F Chain0_4 on A and J, and the silent image is all of
   Chain0_4 (excess 0). (i') and (ii') pass 25/25 for each gamma. On B and I
   the numbers are the R0 deficits exactly. Every expectation E1-E5 held, and
   no number deviates.
Q2 (Q') + (B) reduction and the R1 BCond. MEASURED.
   (Q'): R(ker own) with initial zero is 1,076, and Chain0_4 is inside it on A and J.
   The annihilator of bat(ker own) is exactly the 48 R1 verifier relations
   (384 limbs), so B0 = 1,336 - 384 = 952.
   BCond(R1): bat(ker own ∩ ker R) = bat(ker own) = 952 on every job
   (including B and I, where the deficit is entirely in Q').
   The reduction therefore transfers with the R1 relation set
   {ia, S2a, S2b, S1, C_u, L_u} in place of {ia, S1, S2, S3_u}.
   As in R0, this is evidence on 40 jobs (5 challenge vectors x 2 gammas x 4
   structures). It is not a mass bound over the query draw: B-open and
   OT-open stay premises for R1.
Q3 (D23 on R1). MEASURED.
   * The low shadow (Psi_lo) has no R1 analogue. Its R1 replacement is the exact
     law f0 = f1 = f2 = 0 on D-high: with no chord quotient, D's natural
     coefficients < 56 feed only q1_0..13 and so only final_0..2. final_3 is
     free on V_D (rank 8).
   * etaRel has no R1 analogue. V_G = B0 = 952, and its annihilator is
     exactly the 48 relations. The R0 conjugate relation needs the whole
     once-folded message in the view; R1 discloses q1 only through round1,
     88 folded values and 64 final values.
   * V_D = B0 ∩ {f0 = f1 = f2 = 0} = 952 - 24 = 928 (measured equality).
     V_G = B0. B0 = V_D + V_G, which is trivial for R1 because V_G alone is B0.
   * PCS block (the Z8b analogue): rank 1,304 of 1,712 (batched 1,336 + D own 376),
     and bat on ker own_D unbalanced is 932, of which 4 limbs are .inactiveSum
     (the K4-line). It is not full rank, as in R0.
   Consequence for the design. On R1 the GCover part of (B) is not needed in
   its R0 form, because G's own-silent round kernel alone reaches B0. For R1,
   BCond is equivalent to the single statement V_G = B0 (G-only); the D-high
   absorber is not needed. This is measured, not proved.
