2. Transport pi = D18 (SOURCE transport.rs; CHECKED against the checkpoint's d18_transport)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAU = [3,4,2,0,1]. Coefficient base-4 digit d becomes row base-4 digit TAU[d]
(the identity within each digit):
  row(j) = sum_d ((j >> 2d) & 3) << 2*TAU[d]
  coefficient(r) = dig3(r) + 4 dig4(r) + 16 dig2(r) + 64 dig0(r) + 256 dig1(r).
R0 probes Z8/Z8b/Z8c/Z8d/Z8e used D13 (aspis_core::r0::transport
ROW_TO_COEFFICIENT). Layout consequences, computed from the probe's ACTIVE
table (810 inactive rows; row 0 inactive):
  * coefficient 0 sits at row 0, so row 0 is a D-tail row (under D13, row 0 is coefficient 89);
  * of D's 56 tail rows (coefficients 0..55), only 10 are copy-inactive and 46 are active
    (under D13 all 56 were inactive).

3. Masks and balancing (SOURCE: aspis-prover state_only_entropy.rs
   derive_pool_v1_zero_factor_d; r1 prover.rs prove_masked_with_attempt audit)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
D is uniform in QM31 at every row, with the first inactive row (row 0) set to
minus the sum of the other inactive rows. G, H1 padding and the 10 mask-only
columns are audited to have inactive sum zero. The 16 semantic C1 columns are
balanced with eligible noise. Each mask lane's word space is therefore the
inactive-sum-zero hyperplane, which is the Lean `balance 0` space; which row
carries the balance does not change the span. aspis-prover
state_only_hiding.rs/state_only_entropy.rs and aspis-core
state_only_hiding.rs/state_only_prefix.rs are byte-identical between
v8-reference (524e1debb) and the spend-path tip. aspis-core r0/*, field.rs
and wide_exact.rs differ only by inlining and refactors (diff reviewed). The
aspis-statement semantic-terminal diff is a refactor (bounded_append_carry,
an unrolled serializer, tests).

5. Disclosed view (SOURCE verifier.rs View::parse_shape, prover.rs prove_from_semantics)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per lane l (own observations; the same coordinate types as R0, and in R1
also K4-valued for K4 lanes):
  claims[p][l], p = 0,1,2: MLE of the row word at the three statement points
          (row-indexed; independent of pi)
  extra[l] = sum_{s<2} sum_{k<28} coeff_l[28s+k] * alpha_{8+s}^k  (coefficient-indexed)
  before_z1[l] = eval_message(coeff_l, z0);  y1[l] = eval_message(coeff_l, z1)
  opened: 22 queried fibres x 4 codeword symbols (Enc of coefficients, the R0 encoder)
  -> 3 + 1 + 2 + 88 = 94 values per lane (112 limbs for an F lane, 376 for a K4 lane).
Semantic rows 15..24 (round polynomials), the eta row (H) and the claims are
the semantic layer and are unchanged from R0.
Batched (gamma-weighted across lanes; q = sum_l gamma^l coeff_l, q in E^1024):
  inactive  = sum_j q_j * [row(j) inactive]                      1 E   (0 on balanced masks)
  round0    = round(q, w)                                         7 E   (all 7 sent; R0 sends 6)
  folded word values: q1 = fold(q, alpha0) in E^256; folded_word = evaluate(q1)
            over the 2^18 line nodes 2x_u^2-1; opened at positions 4*(u/4)+s, s<4,
            per query                                             88 E  (if the 22 u/4 are distinct)
  round1    = round(q1, w1),  w1 = dual fold(w, alpha0)           7 E
  final     = fold(q1, alpha1)                                    64 E
  -> 167 E values = 1,336 M31 limbs (R0: 264 E = 2,112 limbs).
The Merkle roots and paths are hashes, and with private-leaf-salts the
salts are not linear coordinates. They are outside this linear model, as in Z8.
Here w = sum_{kind<7} kappa^kind raw(kind, j) (weights.rs materialize):
  kind 0 inactive indicator; kinds 1..3 eq(point_k, row(j)) as a digit tensor;
  kind 4 Libra weight on coefficient j < 56; kinds 5 and 6 natural-basis evaluation of
  coefficient j at z0 and z1.
round(q, w): c[s+PERM[t]] += q[4d+s] w[4d+t] / 4, PERM = [0,3,2,1].
fold(q, a): h0 + a h1 + a^2 h2 + a^3 h3. The dual fold is (h0 + a h3 + a^2 h2 + a^3 h1)/4.
The masks enter the batched coordinates only through q, that is, through
gamma^l times lane l's coefficient vector. There is no quotient.

7. Differences from the R0 probe model (Z8/Z8c/Z8e)
---------------------------------------------------
 D1  pi: D18 (R1) against D13 (R0 probes).
 D2  There is no chord quotient. R0 batches Q(c_l) = (c_l - interpolant)/chord line and uses
     tau, e1 and e2. R1 batches raw coefficients and puts the OOD into weight kinds 5 and 6.
 D3  Two folds: round1, the folded root, 88 folded-word values and alpha1 are new.
     The final message has 64 values, not 256.
 D4  round0 sends all 7 coefficients (R0 sends 6 and implies c4).
 D5  The challenge rows are gamma, kappa, alpha0, alpha1 and rho (QM31). R0 has tau.
 D6  The batched space is 1,336 limbs (R0: 2,112). The relation count is 48 E (R0: 25).
 D7  The weight layout is kappa^0..6 over (inactive, 3 points, Libra, z0, z1).
     In R0 it is indicator + kappa^1..3 eq + kappa^4 libraWeight, then the quotient
     weights + tau e1 + tau^2 e2.
 D8  The D-tail rows are mostly copy-active under D18 (46 of 56). Row 0 is coefficient 0.
 D9  Expected R1 forms of the D23 objects:
     Psi_lo, the R0 low shadow of D-high through the chord quotient, has no R1 analogue,
     because there is no quotient. With no quotient, D-high (coefficients < 56 zero)
     gives q1_d = 0 for d < 14, hence final_0..final_2 = 0 exactly (24 limbs), and final_3 is free.
     etaRel (R0 Fact 3) needs the whole once-folded message to be disclosed. In R1 only 159 of
     the 256 E-functionals of q1 are disclosed, so I expect no conjugate relation
     (to be measured).

8. Checked by the Z9 probe (FINDINGS-Z9.txt sections 1 and 2)
-------------------------------------------------------------
D18 equals the inverse of S2 row. The probe's ACTIVE table equals
pool_v1_pair_forest_copy_active_row_masks_v1. Weight kinds 1-6 equal the
probe's eq/h_weight/evalrow functionals. The folded word is
EncodingDomain::evaluate. The whole-column R1 pipeline equals the row-wise
sum on all 167 coordinates. On a full view, S2a (kappa claim), S2b, S1
(Weights::pair = double dual fold), C_u and L_u all hold. On own-silent
tapes the 48 relations are exactly the annihilator (dim B0 = 952). The 22
queried fibres had 22 distinct u/4 on every vector.
