The release runtime premise consists of exactly two semantic statements:
1. sol_sha256 computes FIPS 180-4 SHA-256 of concatenated input slices.
2. At its derivation step, sol_create_program_address computes one SHA-256
   of concatenated seeds, followed by program_id and ASCII
   "ProgramDerivedAddress", and rejects an on-curve digest. The stored bump
   is an ordinary seed; no search occurs. Admitted calls use at most five
   seeds, each at most 32 bytes, within SDK seed limits 16/32. Generic
   invalid-seed checks can return before hashing: their contribution is zero.

The proof has no Agave-version parameter. At release, record the target
cluster's observed Agave version and source references for these two syscalls.
Those observations are PENDING, not performed by this update. Local 4.2.1
pins/replays remain test evidence, not a release selection or live attestation.
FIPS functional equality does not itself assert random-oracle behavior.

Concrete reference paths, already pinned by the audit: solana-syscalls 4.2.1
src/lib.rs:843-880 (SyscallCreateProgramAddress) and 2540-2600 (SyscallHash);
solana-address 2.6.1 src/syscalls.rs:392-447 (create_program_address).
These references explain the contract but do not identify a future cluster.
Seed/memory/compute checks can stop before hashing; PDA output-memory failure
can occur after hashing. On-curve rejection must retain that digest event.
Hasher updates for individual slices are not separate complete SHA queries.

Existing interface: AspisR1.VerifierCallBudget.PrefixRelation retains complete
(Bytes, Digest) traces and explicit rejection, panic, allocation, compute and
runtime failures. AspisR1.VerifierPhaseCost.SourceSimulation.preservesPrefix
must preserve those traces into ModelPrefix. Its instantiation remains OPEN:
the two syscall premises do not prove other callees SHA-free, source lowering, callback resolution,
allocator/drop/observer effects, or failure-prefix preservation. No axiom or
runtime implementation instance is introduced. Init/governance bump searches
are adversary-side QA under the lead decision, outside invocation QV.
