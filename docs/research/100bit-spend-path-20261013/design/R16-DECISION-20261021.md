## Required repair

The protocol must own the literal constructor schedule before R19 can be
specialised. `CanonicalLiteralProof.liftProver` is an existing candidate:
it constructs messages by row from a decoded wire proof and proves their
validity. R19 currently quantifies over arbitrary `RawCutProverCompiler.Prover`,
not only such canonical outputs. Restricting that quantifier, replacing the
protocol/message carrier, or canonicalising in the verifier/compiler is a
further model change; it is not merely specialising d while carrying every
other parameter/premise verbatim. Existing Lean modules may not be edited by
this worker. A new endpoint with a canonical prover carrier would need an
explicitly reviewed signature specialisation and its byte-to-data and
sampler/refinement proof, not a claim that arbitrary P satisfies R16.

The checkpoint model is deliberately typed storage. It does not claim a
frozen byte serializer, authenticated runtime persistence, complete verifier
acceptance, R13/R14/R3, or an honest complete spend proof. Its non-vacuity
covers exactly the construction-owned first-match checks.
