# Remaining obligations for the 100-bit private-spend proof

Current integrated proof frontier: `506224f37`, with the checked conditional canonical-input adapter also available as a focused worker result. This checklist supersedes older documents where raw-byte decoder recovery, adaptive public selection or the generic Fiat-Shamir reduction are still described as unproved.

The actual all-33-round raw-byte background decoder and the conditional adaptive membership reduction are checked. `RawAdaptiveMembershipBound.membership_bound` still takes `hformat`, `hterminal` and `hQ`. `LiteralFreshAttemptBound.thirty_three_rows_lt` proves the concrete fresh-attempt classifier-hit bound below `2^-100`; it does not by itself bound accepted false spends in the deployed program.

## Core soundness and source correspondence

1. **Actual Rust execution to the oracle program.** Complete the concrete Digest32/backend refinement through the real constructor and all 33 caller rows, including row-26 reseeding and the row-31 selected query state. Prove exact hash-input bytes, call order, returned errors, state updates and acceptance behavior for the admitted entrypoints. A candidate interface or generic hash dictionary is insufficient. Source-to-model sampler correspondence remains part of this work.

2. **Actual decoded inputs and strict formats.** Connect actual source decoding to the canonical decoded-wire prover. Constructor validity and tag 2 are now proved for that adapter, but its input has not been identified with the actual accepted caller's input. Connect the isolated extracted row-2 parser theorem to the exact consumed bytes. Prove the actual row-30 parser yields `OpeningRecordCodec.decode .second` success. Complete the relevant canonical M31/QM31/WideExact/public-byte representation links at their actual use sites.

3. **Public statement and Pool binding.** Derive decoding and serializer success in the real caller, bind the transition to the decoded `lane_transition`, and show the mathematical public statement is precisely the one supplied to verification. The checked Withdrawal bridge does not automatically cover payment/transfer. Prove the metadata helper reads the actual AccountInfo wrapper's fields and preserves identity, ownership, flags, errors and caller reachability.

4. **Authenticated two-phase checkpoint.** Prove tx1's strict parsing/canonicality checks and checkpoint writes authenticate every byte and value trusted by tx2. Prove proof/public/checkpoint identity, finalization and immutability across the admitted lifecycle, including safe provisioning. In particular, derive canonicality of every wide-field value used by the shortened tx2 path from the checked tx1 data and authenticated binding.

5. **Source openings to commitment-time words.** The mathematical raw-log recovery and stability results are checked. Instantiate them with the actual source's C1, C2 and folded Merkle verification: exact leaf bytes, salts, domains, roots, indices, path geometry, padding, consumed values and trace inclusion. Prove accepted source openings equal the typed recovered words at their commitment cuts; default completion alone does not supply those equalities.

6. **Complete accepted-source terminal implication (`hterminal` / D3).** Prove acceptance supplies every field of `LiteralAcceptanceTerminal.Checks`. This includes public parsing; matching/opening readers; actual seven opening functionals; pairing and both fold equations; query decoding and matching support; rho residual batching; semantic claims/polynomials; and sumcheck equations. Compose them with the existing model theorem yielding `Rspend`, including input membership, secret/nullifier relations, bounded conservation, output commitments and public-option shape. Existing joint-list, second-fold lift and algebraic binding theorems are reused; their correspondence to actual source payloads remains necessary.

   The current terminal source subwork still includes the newly emitted indexed reconstruction and 33-cell loops, preprocessing/tail-write composition, the full `add_value_lanes` caller, `add_preweighted`/copy/24 semantic lanes, Poseidon/full-state residual production, claim-array producers and enclosing terminal validation. Extraction success is not a source correctness theorem.

7. **Concrete sampler execution correspondence.** The selected mathematical sampler densities are proved. Connect the actual source challenge construction, reduction, nonzero-gamma handling and state carry to those selected samplers at every row. This is covered by neither a field identity nor an isolated sampler component alone.

8. **Universal query bound (`hQ`).** Prove the verifier's distinct-query bound for every admitted input, including rejected inputs, both transactions, terminal/checkpoint hashes and relevant SDK/PDA calls. Keep the adversary's query budget symbolic and compose it with this verifier bound. Fixture measurements and the candidate `QV = 2250` are not a universal source/runtime proof.

## Knowledge and zero knowledge

9. **Efficient knowledge extractor.** Replace specification-level witness choice/log representatives with an explicit uniform full-log algorithm. Prove complete list decoding and exact list refinements for the initial code, first fold and repeated-lift second fold; deduplication/joint candidate combination; recovery of a witness checked by `Rspend`; expected polynomial field/hash-log/bit cost; and extractor failure `deltaE <= 2^-120`. Membership soundness does not establish knowledge of an existing note's secret.

10. **Zero knowledge for the actual published protocol.** Connect the exact source mask distribution and all disclosed payloads, salts, commitments and openings to the privacy model. Prove the needed mask-image/rank statements and dual-pad/chain corrections, with explicit exceptional-challenge bounds. Construct an efficient public-statement-only simulator. Prove commitment hiding and the adaptive random-oracle real/simulated-view reduction. If publication uses rank rejection/retries, implement and prove the accepted/aborted transcript law, fresh-entropy/nonce discipline and bounded-abort/completeness contract. Finite rank experiments and a Classical.choose simulator do not close these obligations.

## Final composition and the requested spend

11. **Final quantitative theorem.** Instantiate the source-facing premises and combine the exact per-attempt soundness terms, the single shared collision charge and the extractor failure budget under the clarified security experiment. State privacy error separately with its justified terms. Retain the query-dependent repeated/adaptive-attempt statement; no work/grinding credit or claim that arbitrarily many oracle attempts preserve absolute `2^-100` error. Explicitly state the random-oracle and trusted Solana-runtime preconditions.

12. **One coherent checked source/artifact closure.** Integrate the proved candidate source changes into the admitted verifier, resolve cached/maintained interface differences and declaration aliases, and perform the final pinned Lean 4.32 manifest replay once. The theorem closure must contain no `sorry` or unproved external contracts. Pin the Rust source, features, compiler/toolchain and final program artifact to the theorem's actual scope. Prior component receipts do not prove the whole final closure.

13. **Full private-spend release gates.** For the original goal, also prove/check Pool settlement binding, nullifier uniqueness, custody/authorities, output handling and rejection/rollback behavior; settle provisioning after lifecycle-route restrictions; and verify the final proved artifact completes verification plus settlement in at most two transactions within the CU limits using fresh full masks. These settlement/deployment gates are distinguished from the algebraic soundness theorem in the lead decisions. Earlier two-transaction measurements remain evidence for their pinned artifacts, not automatically for new source candidates.
