length” must quantify over that caller, not over `candidate::tx1` or the

The corrected candidate statement is: all transcript/commitment reads are

## Privacy premises and what is discharged

The names in D52.2 do not mean “input has a tag”. Against the actual binary
privacy definitions:

| Premise | Actual meaning | Contribution of the tx1 sites |
|---|---|---|
| `eCm : W.Cm ≃ (RIdx → OracleAnswer)` | A bijective carrier identification, quantified by `RComLog` (`R1Z/EventsR1PBin.lean:111`) | Root/salt/node carriers and slice widths are inputs to this identification. No individual hash site proves a bijection, and this packet constructs none. |
| `ROpen` | Coverage plus two deduplicated batch lists, each without duplicates, whose fibre unions agree (`R1Z/EventsR1PBin.lean:54`) | The three leaf sites and three binary-parent branches are stage-1 batch-A read candidates. Their shapes alone do not establish coverage, ordering, deduplication or the stage-2 batch. No full `ROpen` field is discharged here. |
| `RComLog` | Equality of `W.comLog` with the mapped prover opening list, including oracle addresses **and answers**, for every quantified witness/tape/challenge (`R1Z/EventsR1PBin.lean:111`) | Leaf/node constructions identify the addresses whose exact source log still needs transport. Roots are bound in initializer/row 2/row 30. A typed input without answer/carry and log equality does not discharge `RComLog`; it remains open. |

Semantic initializer/rows, opening reseed/rows and read/advance sites supply
transcript framing and length obligations used by D79's
`TranscriptNodeLengths` specialization. They are not commitment-log reads
that independently discharge one of those three premises. The checkpoint
proof/ASF8/checksum sites belong to R15 and the external observer/ROM read
budget; putting them into `W.comLog` without changing its definition would
be wrong. Seed, salt-generation and expander reads are prover commitment
phase sites, outside the tx1 verifier cone; this inventory does not claim
completeness of `RCommitBytes` or the prover's `RComLog` from their absence.

Thus **none of the three complete D52.2 premises is newly discharged**.
The packet supplies exact retained parser-to-duplex pieces and a complete
source-reading inventory for composing those premises later. This is also
why no theorem named “every tx1 digest input is one of the requested
classes” is introduced.
