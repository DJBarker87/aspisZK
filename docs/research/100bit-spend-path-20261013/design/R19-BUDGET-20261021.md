## Stage 1: literal sampler calls

`R19BudgetSamplerReads.query_tail_length` proves symbolically that n paired
squeeze/advance steps issue 2*n queries. `sampler_length` handles every row
and every message constructor, including invalid inputs: ordinary rows issue
3, initial/reseed rows 4, invalid rows 1, and the query row 17. Its uniform
upper bound is 17. `row_length` transports this result through byte-address
renaming to the actual protocol chain, uniformly in D and oracle H.

Focused attempt 1 passed, three standard-axiom audits, zero swaps. No concrete
large domain, recurrence, generated certificate, or source verifier is evaluated.

## Stage 2: original-experiment composition

`R19BudgetOriginalReads.verifier_queries` proves the actual Lean verifier log
has length at most 33*17 = 561. `verifier_reads` applies the generic
`VerifierCallBudget.firstReads_le_length` for any incoming table, then the
existing equalities `QvStage1.stage1Budget_ten` (595) and
`QvStage2.stage2Budget_twelve` (566). Thus its first reads are at most 1161.
This deliberately conservative estimate avoids evaluating any transcript.

`prefix_queries` inducts on arbitrary prefix logs. It bounds first reads of
pre++post by the issued prefix queries plus suffix first reads in the table
left by pre. `original_reads` unfolds the experiment's log concatenation and
applies `hqueries H` and `verifier_reads`. It holds for every oracle without
acceptance or BoundaryEvidence, and proves exactly the old R19 inequality.

The existing QvStage1/2 CountedTrace and SourceCoverage bridges concern source
call logs, not this literal verifier. No source join is required for the
literal R19 statement: its verifier is precisely the 33-row sampler schedule,
and its decision/decoder maps are pure. Rust log completeness, rejected or
suspended source prefixes, and observer/PDA coverage are not proved here.
They must not be inferred from deletion of this Lean experiment premise.

Focused attempt 1 failed only at the final simplification identifying the
protocol's row count with 33 and 33*17 with 561. Replacing that closing
simplification with definitional equality yielded attempt 2, exit 0, five
standard-axiom audits, zero swaps. Failed error-recovery sorryAx reports are
retained as failed evidence and excluded from accepted audits.
