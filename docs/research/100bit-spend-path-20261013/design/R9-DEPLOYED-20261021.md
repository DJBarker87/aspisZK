## Meaning of deployed

Here `deployed` means the explicit R9 two-transaction oracle-program model:
tx1 either rejects or writes checkpoint bytes, Pool invokes its supplied nested
tx2 continuation with precisely those same bytes, and both transactions use
one memoized oracle. Acceptance is the resumed RunView verdict, and failure
means the binary recorded-public extractor returns `none` on that exact
RunView with independent private extractor coins. This defines a probability
without a free rational. It does not yet identify the supplied continuation
with the frozen Solana Pool/verifier caller: P2′ persistence alone and R20's
conditional field-copy agreement do not establish that identification.
