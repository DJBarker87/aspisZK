## The theorem in words

`R1FinalSoundness.final_soundness` in `lean/R1FinalSoundness.lean` composes
terminal source facts through `DecoderInstanceBoundary.hchecks_deployed_partial`,
then applies `RawExtractorCostComposedV2.recorded_extractor_cost_and_failure`
(which applies `checked_acceptance_failure_bound_no_model`). It proves the
recorded extractor's accepted-run expected cost is at most the explicit
`p.attemptBudget`, every returned witness satisfies `LiteralProtocol.relation`,
and the combined bank/query-scan allowance `2^-126 + 2^-454 ≤ 2^-120`. The relation is the actual parsed
public `Rspend`, including AppendBinding, not a stronger positivity claim.

For the binary format it separately constructs an adaptive injection with the
**proved** five-reader collision mass `6·(Q+1161)^2/256^32`, assumes the still-open
binary completion and clean-word/extractor alignment laws, and applies the
existing collision + decoded-start + bank theorem. A named deployed probability
transport law then adds the query-scan failure probability, bounded by
`QueryScanModelFailure.exhaustion_bound`. The resulting per-attempt bound is

```
F(Q) = 6·(Q+1161)^2/2^256 + 33·(15/2^109) + 2^-126 + 2^-454.
```

Here Q is the adversary/designated-external-read allowance and 1,161 is the
verifier-core allowance. The total experiment budget is Q+1,161. No grinding
credit is applied. The 33 recognized decoder starts are a separate explicit
experiment restriction, not a consequence of a first-read budget. For a
multi-attempt adversary, neither that restriction nor the per-attempt result
may be silently reused as a whole-experiment bound (TARGET-CLARIFICATION).

**Important type check:** M9 and M10 still use the *old* coefficient 12.
The theorem preserves that historical model bound as a separate conclusion;
it does not divide it by two. `h_Rwire` and `h_R12_binary_words` expose the
missing binary consumer bridges identified by BIN-COMMITMENT-MODEL §6. The
binary collision mass alone does not supply them.

`D`, `d` and `source` remain parameters. No deployed decoder is invented or
inferred from verifier behavior: the checked decoder-underdetermination
counterexample still applies. `deployedFailure : ℚ` is an externally supplied
mass of the event “the admitted stage-1 and Pool/nested-stage-2 pair accepts
but extraction of a witness for that same public Rspend fails.” The precise
Lean obligation connecting that mass to the compiled experiment is
`h_R9_transport`; assigning this name to an arbitrary number does not supply
its deployed interpretation. The theorem does not itself define a Solana
probability space or prove the source simulation.

## Numeric instance and exact maximality

`lean/R1FinalSoundnessNumeric.lean` proves, over exact rationals,

```
F(Q) < 2^-100  ↔  Q ≤ 22482956455467539568187.
```

Thus the largest total allowance is **22482956455467539569348**;
after reserving 595+566=1161 verifier reads the largest adversary allowance
is **22482956455467539568187**. `allowance_identity` relates the two.
`endpoint_lt` proves the strict inequality at this endpoint;
`successor_not_lt` proves the bound is ≥2^-100 at the next integer;
`totalBound_mono` extends both results to every smaller/larger allowance.
This is the largest Q certified by **this conservative ledger**, not a claim
that actual failure attains its upper bound. The final theorem's last
conjunct gives the deployed bound for every Q up to that endpoint under the
listed hypotheses. No floating-point rounding or square-root axiom is used.

Equivalently, the largest total integer T is characterized by

```
T² < (2^256/6)·(2^-100 - 495/2^109 - 2^-126 - 2^-454).
```

This calculation charges query-scan once and bank abort once. QueryScan is
**64 words in eight blocks**, not 64 blocks of eight words; the misleading
shorthand in one lead addendum is corrected by QUERYSCAN's source map and
the actual `Fin 64` definition. There is no 512-word experiment here.
If the oracle experiment additionally designates Pool/adapter/PDA/runtime
reads, their separately proved allowance must be charged against Q; 1161
is only the verifier-core allowance (QV-STAGE2). No old PDA constant or
measured fixture count is silently included.
