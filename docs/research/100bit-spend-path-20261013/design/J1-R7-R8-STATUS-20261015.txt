2. R7: unrestricted oracle and conditional theorem

AllAddressFiatShamir.soundness_bound removes the finite-address assumption
from the R0C.V3 first-bad argument. Its experiment law is memoized lazyMean
on Program I A with arbitrary I and finite A. LazyInj retains raw-address
first-cell decoder recovery and a collision-mass premise. Its proof reuses
R0C.V3.lemmaA3, hitsChain3_of_first, FS.exists_flip and the finite potential
support law. An internal extension outside a finite program's potential
support is unobservable; it does not merge long inputs or limit oracle calls.

LiteralByteProtocol.protocol has address type Bytes, 33 rows, and explicit
decoder/extractor parameters. transcript_eq and erased_transcript identify
its protocol-format sampler values with the bounded construction pulled back
along Addr.toBytes. chainsRead and readsChains concern this ideal chain-running
verifier. conditional_bound quantifies arbitrary raw-byte prover and verifier
programs and yields Q*maxErr(eps,33)+kappa(Q), under D1, chain density, D3,
ReadsChains, LazyInj and the distinct-first-read budget.

No claim transports decoder injection through Addr.toBytes: it is not injective
on the whole padded carrier. The legacy CanonicalByteHash fallback module is
not used by the new theorem or oracle. The desired 12*Q^2/2^256 collision mass
must still be proved in this raw-byte experiment. Neither D1--D3 nor efficient
extraction is supplied by a parameterized theorem.
