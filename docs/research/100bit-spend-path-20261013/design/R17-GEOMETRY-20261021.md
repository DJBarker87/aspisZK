## Symbolic closed forms and index geometry

`lean/R17GeometryClosedForms.lean` adds ten audited theorems. `walk_closed`
proves an emit-before-advance monoid recurrence by induction at symbolic i;
`exponent_walk` identifies its offset/stride power. The two table recurrence
lemmas identify the existing model's `tableA`/`tableB` with these walks in
bit-reversed order. `masked_tables_eq_stored` proves that the literal source
mask/shift lookup specification gives the stored log-20 fibre point at every
symbolic index. `natural_child`, `natural_eq_stored`, `natural_injective`
connect the literal four-arm core natural-index match to the actual model.
`literal_lookup_to_stored` retains the two table-content hypotheses explicitly;
the optimized executable certificate supplies byte evidence, not a Lean term
for those hypotheses.
