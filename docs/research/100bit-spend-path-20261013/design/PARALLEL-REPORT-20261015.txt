Source corrections and cross-proposal constraints

1. Decoder dispatch categories are not the mathematical challenge schedule.
UnifiedSampler routes row 0 through initialization and row 26 through
reseed. The source r1_wire.rs/r1_semantic.rs and
TRANSCRIPT-R1-INVENTORY.md instead identify semantic field challenges at
rows 0--24 (lambda at 0), circle challenges at 25/26, gamma/kappa/alpha0/
alpha1 at 27--30, q22 at 31, and rho at 32. No zero-error initialization
or reseed row follows from the decoder labels.

2. ConcreteBinding's caps are for fixed words and prefix data. An existential
union over unknown words does not inherit those caps. Causal recovery fixes
words on an actual trace outside a hash event; it does not make an arbitrary
table-dependent classifier satisfy FS2.D2. D2 quantifies independently over
T' and T. D2TableBoundary.table_flip_cost proves epsilon >= 1 if a change of
table makes every challenge escape. A needs C's commitment-time word
interface and B's extraction-failure event before the raw classifier can be
fixed. A membership-only classifier cannot silently acquire knowledge D1.

3. FS2.D3 is unconditional in H and T. C's recovery lemmas require absence
of CombinedMerkleCollision.Coll. The proposed D3Outside/theorem43_outside
extension must be proved; it is not an existing specialization. Inspection
of R0C.V3.Gen.lemmaB3 shows that it already receives the no-collision
premise locally, which gives a specific proof route for the extension.

4. FS2.extractFails reads the table before the first address of row 0.
B's proposed extractor uses the full first-read log, including later C2 and
folded commitments. These are different events. A new event-containment
and density argument is required; merely replacing the extractor parameter
in UnifiedFiatShamir.soundness_bound does not prove B's theorem.

5. UnifiedOracleInj.inj uses CombinedMerkleCollision.budget =
12*Qtot^2/256^32. CombinedMerkleCollision.mass proves this under universal
hQ, and old_included contains the old event. Do not retain the pure-decision
3*Qtot^2 term or add it again. Additional events require separate accounting
if not shown included. The 583 raw/452 distinct figures in the existing
measurement are one successful verifier run, not a full-experiment bound.

6. CanonicalByteHash.encode sends overlength inputs to the empty address.
The source model needs a length bound for whole proof, public and checkpoint
hash inputs as well as leaves/nodes. verifier.rs explicitly requires an
authentic tx1 checkpoint and protected proof bytes in tx2/tx2_authenticated;
an unkeyed checkpoint checksum does not prove account provenance.

Proposal review

A — CLASSIFIER.txt gives an existence/no-earlier-hit classifier on a proposed
enriched literal-word view, explicit D1/D2/D3 contracts, malformed-input
branches and all row events. It does NOT provide the requested concrete raw
UnifiedProtocol instance: the word-view adapter is explicitly OPEN. The
semantic route uses R0P.Mask.semantic_round_densityZ and early_candidate_to_prefixZ;
the latter retains the pre-C2 padding argument rather than fixing C2 before
lambda/chi. ConcreteBinding.{gammaBad_card,kappaBad_card,firstBad_card,
secondBad_card} supply the four caps; the fold caps require degree <= 6.
OpeningSamplerBound's four mass lemmas additionally require explicit byte
and field equivalences. QueryResidualBridge.rhoBad_card and
RhoSamplerBound.modulo_bad_mass give the rho row. The q22 bound applies only
to a fixed matching set. SemanticPrefix.large_matching_extracts_of_prefix
retains typing, parsed-prefix, transport, sumcheck and exclusion premises.

This is arithmetic evidence, not a compiled maxErr theorem or row closure.

B — KNOWLEDGE.txt specifies a full-log, one-execution knowledge event and
an executable relation rather than witness nonexistence. It proposes GRS
decoding for initial (n,d,t)=(1048576,1024,38230), fold one
(262144,255,9558), and native fold two (64 coefficients, length 65536,
threshold 2390) through repeated lifting into fold one's code. No new
native decoder is claimed. AspisWide.GRSConversion's two threshold transports and the R1
GRSMessageInverse image test support the initial/final interfaces.
ConcreteProjection.exactFinal_sparseLift_child and fullQuartetSet_card
give the repeated-code route. IncrementalCandidateLoop.candidates_eq_lambda
requires complete scalar lists and lane coverage. The 290000 pair bound
additionally requires 29 steps and scalar list sizes <=100; it is not a
runtime bound. The proposed randomized decoder, bit-cost contract and
failure budget are all OPEN. Efficient full-spend witness production is
stronger than SpendSemantics.NoteFacts and requires relation refinement.

C — TERMINAL-D3.txt supplies proposed canonical hash, accepted-opening,
commitment-time word, and D3Outside contracts. The checked recovery names
are CanonicalMerkleRecovery.recover_or_events, the three
ThreeCommitmentRecovery/ThreeCommitmentFullTrace specializations,
CanonicalRecoveryStability.recover_stable, and the C1/LaterTableStability
word-at-first-read theorems. Their prerequisites include consistent logs,
literal commitment frame reads, bounded input lengths, accepted canonical
paths/read inclusion, universal hQ and event exclusions. None directly
identifies Rust payload coordinates, typed words or checkpoint provenance.
The wrapper preserves repeated reads and uses one common collision event.
The proposed outside-D3 theorem is a membership-bound extension; B still
needs its distinct knowledge event and first-read density proof.

Review corrections incorporated: actual challenge/error schedule, explicit
unconditional D3 versus no-event recovery, correct eval namespace, and the
V3 hitsChain3/blaw interface. UnifiedProtocol's own decode is none, so a
knowledge argument cannot use that field's FS2.hitsChain while claiming
reuse of UnifiedDecoder. New helper names in the proposals are obligations,
not existing theorems. No rank or practical extractor-runtime claim passed
this review because neither has been established here.

Single ordered Lean job list — proposed fixed interfaces, all OPEN

Use C's pr,V,v H,pf H,P H,Coll notation. In the following statements E is
the extractor, Wspend the witness type, coins its random tape, and
failE v coins := not (exists w, E pub v.2.1 (firstReadLog v.1) coins =
some w and Rspend pub w). Rspend, the random-tape law and a cost semantics
must be concrete definitions, not opaque proof assumptions.

J1. Freeze the relation and views. Specify Wspend and executable Rspend,
including note membership/secret, nullifier, bounded conservation, output
bindings and canonical options. Define commitment-cut logs, deterministic
default completion and typed prefix parsers. Fix source S2/private-salt
configuration, public-byte encoding, row sigma maps and all input-length
bounds. Prove parser stability under prefix extension. Decide whether the
raw classifier has a justified adapter or knowledge uses a causal event
family; do not claim a table-independent adapter already exists.

J2. Instantiate terminal hashes and source acceptance. Proposed target:
  sourceAccept H input -> modelAccept H input
and, on accepted source executions, equality of literal canonical hash logs.
Prove every authenticated opening-program read is included in v H and
every commitment frame is the literal earlier frame. State checkpoint
provenance/protected-account preconditions explicitly. Bound every input
by L. For an adversary read bound QA and verifier bound QV, target
  forall H, distinctFirstReads (v H) <= QA + QV.
QA, QV remain symbolic until universally justified, including rejected runs;
the 452 fixture measurement is not QV. If exact source/model traces differ
on rejection, prove the required one-way acceptance and read bound instead.

J3. Compose commitment recovery. For each commitment kind k and accepted
query i, prove
  not (Coll (v H)) -> accepts (v H) ->
    recoverAtCut k H (slots k H i) =
      some (openedPayload k H i, openedSalt k H i).
Then prove payload decoding/coordinate equality to one fixed W,V at their
proper cuts, with WordTyped and total-word completion. Reuse the three
table-stability and common-trace recovery lemmas; preserve their size,
consistency, frame-read and event hypotheses. None entries stay explicit
until totalization, and accepted-slot equality must survive totalization.

J4. Refine efficient decoding/extraction. Targets include
  (decode0 w).toFinset = InitialResponses.Close w
  (merge29 W).toFinset = InitialResponses.Lambda W
and exact corresponding fold-code lists, outside the declared decoder
failure event. Prove E-returned witness validity by checking Rspend and
prove the expected bit-cost contract from KNOWLEDGE.txt with one explicit
deltaE budget. Prove sufficient support and candidate completeness; do not
infer them from Merkle acceptance. Refine the inverse/image test and finite
merge into executable representations. No dense interpolation run is part
of this job proposal.

J5. Prove A's row predicates and the chosen failure interface. For the
enriched membership protocol prA, prove the exact D1/D2 contracts printed
in CLASSIFIER.txt and FS.maxErr eps 33 = qcap. D2 ranges over every prefix,
message, unrelated T',T and every completing-sampler context. Its density
arguments must use one fixed set of pre-challenge data, including the
row-31 support and the row-32 residual vector. Prove actual sampler-map
equalities and malformed-case rejection. Transfer to raw UnifiedProtocol
only with the adapter theorem from J1/J3; otherwise keep this membership
result explicitly separate from knowledge.

J6. Prove terminal D3Outside and generic transport. Targets:
  forall H T, not (Coll (v H)) -> rb.doomed (P H) T ->
    (eval H (V pub (pf H))).2 = false
and the same theorem43 conclusion with D3Outside replacing unconditional
D3, retaining D1, ChainDensity3, ChainsRead, ReadsChains, Inj3 and hQ.
Use J3's fixed words and semantic/fold/pairing/accumulator implications,
then UnifiedOracleInj.inj; charge its combined event only once.

J7. Prove the separate knowledge inclusion and density, using
decK := UnifiedDecoder.decode p hD pub and a concrete new badK:
  forall H coins, accepts (v H) -> failE (v H) coins ->
    not (Coll (v H)) -> DecoderComplete (v H) coins ->
    R0C.V3.hitsChain3 decK badK emptyTable (v H).1
  forall a T own pend g, decK a T = some (own,pend,g) ->
    R0C.V3.blaw g
      (fun out => indicator (badK out.1 out.2.1 T out.2.2)) own pend <= epsK.
Prove nonnegative epsK and decoder failure mass <=deltaE. Applying
R0C.V3.lemmaA3 and the combined event mass would then target
  Pr[accepts v and failE v coins] <= Q*epsK + 12*Q^2/2^256 + deltaE.
The equality/bound epsK<=qcap is a separate OPEN obligation. A's membership
D1 and the absence of hash events do not prove either knowledge target.

J8. Complete source/account/settlement correspondence and the one-attempt
experiment theorem, retaining the separate adaptive-Q formula. Only after
focused statements and bridges are green: one axioms audit and one frozen
manifest replay, with exact target/status/time/RSS/swap/revision/axioms
receipts under AGENTS.md. The lead owns the reserved build-host queue;
this proposal authorizes no host job or repeat of unchanged regressions.
