with a model value equation when available. An inner Rust `Err` or `Option.none`

premise, even in a theorem named `total`. OTHER includes complete-result
transport (which may transport failure), algebra alone, inventories and failed
extractions. Typed `Fin`/fixed-array domains are stated explicitly; they do not
cover arbitrary machine indices or arbitrary slices.
