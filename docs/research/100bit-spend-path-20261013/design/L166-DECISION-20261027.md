## What is missing and why
The D188 extraction item gives Result-level fidelity: the extracted
functions return the same Result as the shipped code. L166 needs the
attempted digest-call SEQUENCE (including on rejected paths), i.e.
trace-level fidelity. The attempted-call recorder is not observable from a
Result-valued extraction, and the whole-body Kani certificate that would
supply it does not fit the solver at the actual bounds.
