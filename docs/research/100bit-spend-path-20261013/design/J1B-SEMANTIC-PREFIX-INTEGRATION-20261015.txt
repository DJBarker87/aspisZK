1. Proposed boundary

LiteralSemanticCodecBridge.lean projects completed record bytes from literal
messages. An initial C1 message projects to PublicInitFrame.emptyRecord;
payload/C2 messages retain their bytes. Constructor validity is checked by
the existing literal reader, not inferred from that total projection.

row_zero_record_of_decode proves the exact reverse byte equality needed for
row zero: strict semantic decode at row zero implies the five zero bytes.
This respects the binding warning: the 25 payload records include the empty
row-zero record, while literal/Unified row zero carries C1 and synthesizes
that record. No one-position shift is introduced.

decodeFrom_of_read converts any successful literal semantic read to the
accepted SemanticPrefixCodec decode of the projected records. full_of_read
adds length 25, constructs a dependent payload vector, and supplies exact
re-encoding. read_prefix_stable preserves parsed message/challenge pairs
under successful completed-record extension.

LiteralSemanticPayloadBridge.lean supplies the converse representation
bridge. Given constructor-valid literal messages, successful decodeFull of
their record bytes, explicit ordinary challenge/state coordinates, and the
C2 word carried at row two, read_of_decodeFull_atC2 yields exactly
SemanticPayloadView.pairs for that vector. lifted_read_of_decodeFull gives
the old semantic rounds. No future recovery callback is introduced: the
callback is the pair of lanes of that row-two literal word. pair_c2Base and
pair_c2Words connect it to LiteralTypedContext, so the existing
SemanticPayloadOpeningView.opening_view_of_payloads is the next consumer;
its circle/opening premises remain explicit.

The constructors, bytes and challenges are representation premises. These
files do not assert actual Rust execution, verifier acceptance, D1–D3,
density, candidate extraction or Merkle recovery. They introduce no global
hash-input length cap; the inherited bounded message carrier is used only
for its existing representation, consistent with lead decision J1.R7.
