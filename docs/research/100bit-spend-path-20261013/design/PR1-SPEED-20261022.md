## Stage 1: shared source descriptors

`convert_shared` retains all converter arithmetic, source materialization and shape checks. It returns the two source-ID sequences already constructed by `family`; `extracted_builders_shared` projects writer descriptors from them instead of calling `family` a second time. All extracted writer calls and the deterministic checker remain unchanged. The audit/reference converter retains its original interface. This first change removes the second source-column reconstruction; it does not skip the converter fields overwritten by extracted writers, because computing them can reject or panic.

For successful conversion, the removed calls receive the same immutable `Lanes`, certificate, and lane maps as the earlier calls. `family` has no mutable state or randomness; every checked index and every `src3` result is the same. Its first invocation and every associated failure remain. Allocation exhaustion is within the retained allocator/std trust boundary (process abort, not catchable arithmetic panic). No arithmetic reduction, accumulator bound or overflow semantics changes. No worker or deterministic merge change in stage 1.

Functional gates passed: three source-bound per-step Kani projection/expected-overflow harnesses; 1,000 seeded challenge-set comparisons (4 accepting full canonical sets, 249 each duplicate-query, out-of-range-query, HalfOK, zero-alpha rank rejection), zero verdict or input-hash mismatches; six fixture proof/public comparisons, all byte-identical; three rebuilt verifier ELFs at the exact release pins. The challenge corpus is measurement-style evidence, heavily weighted toward rejection cases, not uniform or exhaustive sampling.

The first Kani harness hit a `memcmp` unwind assertion. Its replacement using two symbolic descriptor entries was stopped proactively at approximately 4.6 GiB process RSS after roughly two minutes. Neither failure was a source counterexample or memory-pressure kill. The final single-entry per-step proof passed in about four seconds; stable `map` traversal extends that projection to the source sequence. This is a compositional argument, not an unbounded whole-program Kani proof. Failed harness sources and raw receipts are retained.

P_R1 *phase subtotals* were 33.446095 / 33.644702 s and 33.239967 / 33.296189 s. These omit parent cleanup and therefore are not complete P_R1 wall times. Baseline first pair overlapped compilation; candidate first transfer overlapped the first Kani attempt. They establish no reliable causal speedup. A replacement comparison adds a complete parent timer and alternates variants serially, three samples per fixture, with no other task build or proof active. This rerun repairs specifically missing timing evidence and identified contention, rather than repeating an unchanged full regression. Measurements are not universal equivalence proofs. | Fixture | Baseline P_R1 s | Candidate P_R1 s | Baseline end-to-end s | Candidate end-to-end s |

| Transfer | 34.604487 | 34.711612 | 39.123553 | 39.314835 |
| Withdrawal | 34.245026 | 34.220637 | 38.852415 | 38.826793 |

Transfer regressed by 0.107126 s in P_R1 (~0.31%) and 0.191282 s end to end (~0.49%). Withdrawal improved by just 0.024389 s in P_R1 and 0.025622 s end to end. These small differences are within the visible shared-host variation; no reliable speedup is established. In particular, the required improvement on **both** fixtures is absent. The stricter task stop rule applies even though this is below D168's +10% ceiling.

The complete-timer comparison is the performance gate; the earlier confounded child-timer samples are retained as exploratory evidence and are not pooled with it. No unchanged final comparison was repeated. The final comparison timed the complete `p_r1_check` body, including quiet-hook setup, panic catch and cleanup, separately from its child phases. Proof timing covers deterministic full-mask fixture generation, including P_R1; strict tx1/tx2 and dense verification run outside that timer. These fixtures use deterministic private seeds and the fixture nonce store, not fresh OS entropy/durable-ledger retry-tail measurements.

Stages 2 (blocked/SIMD kernels) and 3 (additional workers) were **not attempted**, as required by the stop rule. No new arithmetic kernel, delayed reduction, accumulator bound or worker-merge proof is claimed. A preliminary, unapplied stage-2 editing script was discarded. The stage-1 descriptor-sharing experiment remains committed on this unmerged branch for review; it has not passed the performance gate or been adopted as a speedup. Hard invariants include all early failures, HalfOK, rank failures and panic-to-reject behavior, identical proof bytes, checked overflow and exact deterministic checking.
