## Why the requested ledger closure remains open

`RawExtractorCostComposedBin.LedgerRefinement` demands every actual arithmetic
occurrence (including aborts), a full read budget, and all allocation/parsing/
arithmetic bit work. The new frame mirror discharges comparison work only.
The new cursor discharges structural work conditional on a bounded element
mirror. The GS cell theorem consumes dimension bounds, not the actual
extractor's interpolation/root-recovery scheduling and stored-array invariant.
The live-bit theorem likewise consumes a slot invariant; it cannot supply one.

Still required: public primitive decoders and complete parser composition;
address construction/serialization; consuming cached-list indexing and typed
word decoding; the GS interpolation/normalization/RR/GCD/division/power and
candidate/witness-checking occurrence/size bridge; and full allocation/bit
accounting. These are several independent unproved implementation cuts.
Bundling them into one named “GS” or “continuation” premise would not be the
requested strict narrowing. No such bundled premise or arbitrary resource
assignment is asserted here. In particular, multiplying a partial counter by
512 is not used to claim complete representation adequacy.

The new value-equal eager mirror and the dimension envelope are established;
the complete costed extractor, implementation ledger discharge and requested
