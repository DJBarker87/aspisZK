## Reason and scope

`D0OpeningRecoveryObstruction.depth18_counterexample` proves that under the
zero oracle two different packed leaves successfully open at the same root;
a deterministic recovered leaf cannot equal both. Thus accepted openings
alone cannot justify unconditional source-to-recovered-data identification.
The compiler model's pure erased decision does not perform these hash reads.
The appropriate source joins therefore apply outside the existing binary
collision event, whose probability is already charged by κ.

