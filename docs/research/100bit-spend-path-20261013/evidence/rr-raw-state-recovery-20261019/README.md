# Actual finite-tape polynomial-root recovery from raw rows

An arbitrary RawState whose represented bivariate polynomial is nonzero and whose row polynomials have degree at most ell now has an executable initial normalizer. Its finite scanner cannot return none under those hypotheses. Dropping the leading zero inner-X rows factors a nonzero inner-X power and preserves every polynomial root, including zero. No input validity or row-zero-nonzero premise is assumed.

RRConcreteRawRecovery connects this constructor to the actual finite-tape RR traversal. `recover_correct` proves soundness of every returned candidate and completeness for all roots of degree less than d (including zero), directly for the original RawState model. `recover_cursor_le` bounds actual random-block consumption by d*ell on every tape. `recover_abort_le` bounds abort mass for this same executable recovery by d*ell*2^-t under an independent uniform field-pair tape. Initial normalization and recovery definitions are outside noncomputable sections; invariant proof arguments are erased.

RRConcreteRetryBudget specializes the actual recovery masses for ell=112, t=149 and depths 1025 and 256: the sum of the two individual masses is less than 2^-131, hence within 2^-120. This is not yet a theorem that the full adaptive extractor makes exactly these calls, or a proof of its joint caller failure probability.
