## Record gaps and corrections found

1. `external_linear_lazy` fails with "Unimplemented" under both T0 (PCWP_E001) and T2 (PCXQ_E009), but it is not in the 21-entry CANNOT_EXTRACT table. `pattern_values_windowed` (T2 region-join error, PCXQ_E009) is also missing. Both should be added.

Several deployed-branch callees therefore have no gate at all: `*_factored`, `*_selector_tensor*`, `pattern_values_windowed`, the copy-basis kernels and `public_digest_packed_selector_tensor`. PIN2_TYPEDEP already states that "fresh(PIN2) = gate o drop would be false or unprovable as stated" for 8 of the 13 functions.
3. Finding 27b says `close_checkpoint` is "hand-modelled". No constant of it is in the deployed_splitB cone, and PIN2_MODEL_NOTES assigns its hand model to the Stream A lead as still owed.
4. The FIq equalities for `full_round` and `full_round_m31_constants` bind the opaque `external_linear_lazy` by name to the probe constant (PCWP-C4). Their EQUALITY_PROVED is conditional on question (1).
