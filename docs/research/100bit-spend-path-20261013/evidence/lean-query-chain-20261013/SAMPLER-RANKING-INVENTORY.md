## Implementations

In [R1 `sampler.rs`](../../../../../crates/aspis-r1-cost-probe/src/sampler.rs:8), `qm31_sample` reads a `[u8; 32]` as eight low-to-high little-endian `u32` words, performs four long divisions by `P`, and constructs `QM31` from the four remainders in order `(d0,d1)` and `(d2,d3)`. The helper `low_base_p_digits` performs eight such divisions directly from the unreduced rank. `ordinary_sample` decodes those eight low base-`P` digits; it drops the remaining quotient. `gamma_sample` adds the quotient plus one to the digit vector and, on a final carry, adds one again to account for `P^8 ≡ 1 (mod P^8−1)`.

The frozen core in [`r0_transcript.rs`](../../../../../crates/aspis-core/src/r0_transcript.rs:89) uses the same eight little-endian `u32` input words but a different execution path: `reduce_rank` scans 256 bits and conditionally subtracts the modulus, then `base_p_digits` performs eight long divisions. Its `qm31_sample` reduces by `P^4` and decodes four digits; `ordinary_sample` reduces by `P^8`; `gamma_sample` reduces by `P^8−1`, increments the rank once, and decodes eight digits. These are distinct implementations of the respective modulo maps.

On the Rust representation side, `M31`/`CM31`/`QM31` are declared in [`field.rs`](../../../../../crates/aspis-core/src/field.rs:18), with `QM31 { c0, c1 }` at line 424. [`wide_exact.rs`](../../../../../crates/aspis-core/src/field/wide_exact.rs:5) documents `WideExact = c0 + c1*v`, with each coordinate a QM31; `to_limbs` at lines 236–247 lists the eight order as `c0.c0.a`, `c0.c0.b`, `c0.c1.a`, `c0.c1.b`, then the corresponding four limbs of `c1`. `from_le_bytes` parses each field limb as a little-endian `u32` and delegates canonical-range checking to `from_limbs`.

## Existing Lean rank and field links

- [`R0C.ModuloCounting.reduce`](../../../v8-r0-close-20261007/lean/R0C/ModuloCounting.lean:15) is the abstract function `x.val % n`. `fiber_card_le` bounds the residue fiber by `B/n + 1`; `encoded_mass_le` states the atom bound for explicit equivalences `input : A ≃ Fin B` and `output : Fin n ≃ E`.
- [`R0C.ModuloField.blockRank`](../../../v8-r0-close-20261007/lean/R0C/ModuloField.lean:18) is `State ≃ Fin (256^32)`. `blockRank_value` states its value as `∑ i : Fin 32, (s i).val * 256^i.val`, making byte zero the least-significant byte in the model.
- Mathlib’s `finFunctionFinEquiv` definition and `finFunctionFinEquiv_apply` theorem are in the archived source [`Algebra-BigOperators-Fin.lean`](../../../v8-full-view-zk-20260912/evidence/r406-sampler-word-distribution/mathlib-sources/Algebra-BigOperators-Fin.lean:578). For `f : Fin n → Fin m`, the encoded rank is `∑ i, f(i) * m^i`; the inverse coordinate is `rank / m^i % m`. This is the positional basis used for base-256 byte words and base-`P` limbs.
- [`R406SamplerWordDistribution.word_value`](../../../v8-full-view-zk-20260912/lean/AspisV8R19/R406SamplerWordDistribution.lean:46) proves each four-byte word has value `b0 + 256*b1 + 256^2*b2 + 256^3*b3`. `masked_value` at line 64 relates the low-18-bit word to modulo `2^18`. These results are byte/word ranking facts; they do not state a sampler output equivalence.
- [`R599CanonicalFieldTuple.tupleFieldEquiv`](../../../v8-full-view-zk-20260912/lean/AspisV8R19/R599CanonicalFieldTuple.lean:21) maps `Fin 4 → Fin P` to `QM31Exact`; `digitsField` in [`R0C.ModuloField`](../../../v8-r0-close-20261007/lean/R0C/ModuloField.lean:25) splits eight digits into two groups of four and maps to `WideExact`. `digitsField_apply` at R599 lines 38–40 explicitly yields the pair of `decode4` values. `decode_eq_tuple_iff` at lines 55–92 links a canonical four-element decoded list to its digit tuple.
- [`SamplerFieldDecode.decode4`](../../../v8-full-view-zk-20260912/lean/AspisV8R19/SamplerFieldDecode.lean:10) constructs a `QM31Exact` from the four natural coordinate values. The exact tower aliases identify `M31Exact = ZMod P` and `QM31Exact = QuadraticAlgebra CM31Exact qm31R 0` in [`ExactTowerBase.lean`](../../../v8-full-view-zk-20260912/lean/AspisV8R15/ExactTowerBase.lean:17).
- `R0C.ModuloField.fieldRank` at lines 42–43 is the equivalence `Fin(P^8) ≃ WideExact`, constructed by positional base-`P` decoding followed by `digitsField`. `ordinary` at lines 75–76 is `fieldRank (reduce (blockRank s))`; `gammaNZ`/`gamma` at lines 78–83 apply `nonzeroRank` to reduction modulo `P^8−1`, where `positiveRanks` adds one before field decoding. Existing mass statements are `ordinary_mass`, `gamma_mass`, `ordinary_mass_257`, and `gamma_mass_257` at lines 85–123.

## Relation to `RhoSamplerBound`

[`RhoSamplerBound.qm31_parameter_bad_mass`](../../../100bit-spend-path-20261013/lean/RhoSamplerBound.lean:47) has explicit but abstract `input : A ≃ Fin (2^256)` and `output : Fin(P^4) ≃ K` parameters. It applies `R0C.ModuloCounting.reduce` and `encoded_mass_le` to the residual set. The checked source does not name the Rust `[u8;32]`, `sampler::qm31_sample`, `blockRank`, or a specific `QM31Exact` output equivalence.

The inspected cache contains `R599CanonicalFieldTuple.tupleFieldEquiv` and the generic `finFunctionFinEquiv`, which are candidate parts of a `Fin(P^4) ≃ QM31Exact` map. The inspected files do not provide a named composition that identifies this output equivalence with R1 `qm31_sample`. `R0C.ModuloField` directly models `P^8` ordinary/gamma maps into `WideExact`; it does not define a `P^4` QM31 sampler. Its modulo maps are extensional finite functions rather than operational models of either Rust division implementation.

`R445InitialBlockRejectionLaw` uses a separate rejection-based initial-block route (`run 8` over decoded tape); its presence as a dependency of R599 does not make that rejection sampler the total R1 modulo sampler. `R139SamplerSourceBridge` states the QM31 challenge wrapper/source equality and bounded nonzero-loop unfolding; it is not a theorem connecting R1’s sampler implementation to the rank/field equivalences above.
