## The connected argument

1. **Fixed received components.** `NearGammaSelectedC1.rawBatch` uses the
   actual 29-lane scalar powers: the 26 C1 lanes and arbitrary three late C2
   lanes. Both are fixed before gamma; C1 is separately fixed before C2's
   early challenges.
2. **Code-valued messages, not a decoder filter.**
   `NearGammaMessageCover` lifts the existing near-gamma construction through
   the range of a linear encoder. `NearGammaSelectedC1` supplies the actual
   V7 circle encoder, linearity and complete-fibre overlap bound. It retains
   the small-Good alternative; otherwise it constructs 29 message vectors
   with at least 245,609 jointly agreeing fibres.
3. **Literal coefficients and early fixing.**
   `NearGammaSelectedCoefficients` proves encoder injectivity from the
   retained overlap theorem and symbolic domain cardinality. It connects
   the raw/encoded mismatch orientations and actual four-slot index map.
   Every near original candidate's message equals the batch of the
   constructed tuple. Its C1 projection equals `earlyC1 c1`, despite the
   arbitrary late C2. This is mathematical existence/identification, not
   efficient computation of the tuple.
4. **The actual chord reconstruction, with poles retained.**
   `QuotientOriginalCore` and `SelectedQuotientOriginal` establish
   `encode(original Q)=L*encode(Q)+encode(I)` for the defined image-valid
   reconstruction on the actual stored circle domain. For arbitrary received
   words, totalize `R=(received−encode(I))/L`. A nondegenerate chord has at
   most two zero symbols: its stereographic numerator is a nonzero degree-two
   polynomial and the stored parameters are injective. Thus
   `originalBad ⊆ quotientBad ∪ poleFibres` and
   `#originalBad ≤ #quotientBad+2`. There is no assumed inverse at every
   unqueried position and no probability assigned to the poles.
5. **Why 2,324 here.** The prior geometric theorem supplies a quotient
   anchor at distance at most `4B`, representing every B-close adaptive
   final, or at most three eligible alphas. `4*2324+2=9298≤9301` connects the
   dense image-valid anchor to the constructed original-code tuple. The
   strict geometric margin `5B+255<262144` also holds. Using 2,325 and silently
   dropping poles would not justify this connection.
6. **Actual gamma and repaired row assembly.** `ComponentRows` starts from
   the existing literal affine corrected constructor. Given the now-derived
   original message equality, each point-row discrepancy is the degree-28
   polynomial of individual component errors. The tuple, row covectors and
   individual claims are fixed before gamma; the inactive scalar may depend
   on gamma but precedes kappa.
7. **Joint compact acceptance.** `GammaComponentGame` composes the previous
   geometric/image/row game, with arbitrary causal responses and adaptive
   finals. `SelectedComponentGame.component_game_dichotomy` supplies its
   recovered-close interface using steps 2–5, rather than leaving it as a
   hypothesis. The sparse-Good case charges up to 63 gamma values. Outside
   them, any close image-valid anchor would contradict the defining Good
   predicate, so remaining near acceptance is handled jointly by the
   geometric/image/row argument. Dense wrong component claims charge up to
   28 gamma roots, then the same joint argument.
8. **The two OOD vectors, in the same execution.** `ComponentOODBinding`
   builds the actual natural-circle evaluation as a linear functional on
   message coefficients. Checked interpolation and the chord's two zeros
   imply that an image-valid reconstruction evaluates to the batched OOD
   answers at both circle points, including equal-x interpolation. If that
   reconstruction equals the recovered tuple batch, the corresponding
   individual OOD-error polynomial vanishes at gamma. A wrong individual
   answer gives a nonzero degree-at-most-28 polynomial. Outside its roots,
   a close image-valid anchor is impossible; the joint geometric/image/row
   bound handles the remaining near acceptance. In
   `SelectedClaimGame.all_claims_dichotomy`, one pre-gamma tuple is constructed
   before **both** classes of claims are considered. Selecting one wrong
   claim from the fixed prefix gives the same ceiling, not a sum of 145
   per-claim ceilings. No correct individual OOD answer is assumed.

The game permits scalar query cancellation and later relation repair; it

## Total accounting, including what is not bounded

Let `A` be the intended complete repaired verifier's acceptance and `X` a
specified resource-bounded extractor returning a checked payment witness.
The target remains `Pr[A ∧ ¬X]`. The following is an obligation map, not a
claim that the complete source-to-game partition has been proved.

| Precedence / class | Current treatment |
|---|---|
| Authentication, source mismatch, parser failure, replay/fuel abort, missing responses, cached/advance challenge mismatch | Separate source/extractor coupling; no numerical error assigned |
| Actual final farther than 2,324 from folded R | Accepted **extraction failure** remains unbounded; far acceptance alone is not failure |
| Near final, sparse original-code Good | All compact field-game acceptance bounded here, including absent mathematical early C1 |
| Near final, dense Good, wrong individual ordinary or OOD component point claim | Same constructed tuple and joint gamma/image/row composition give the local bound |
| Near final, dense Good, all component point claims correct | Need early semantic/copy enforcement and the checked payment endpoint; not charged as already solved |
| Witness successfully extracted and independently validated, in any radius | Zero contribution to `A ∧ ¬X` |

The last two classes prevent rebranding mathematical tuple existence as
knowledge extraction. The C1-only identification is useful for early
semantic challenge arguments, but a tuple fixed before gamma after adaptive
C2 is not itself frozen before lambda/chi. Those arguments must explicitly
use its identified early C1 projection and keep their causal probability
accounting.

The totalized oracle at poles is a mathematical over-approximation of a
source path that rejects an opened zero denominator. A completed byte-source
refinement still needs that abort implication. No source equality at those
rejecting executions is asserted here.
