## Why the composition does not pay a family query union

The actual relation-compatible moment is the matching-query probability
multiplied by the indicator that the **actual carried prior** is zero.

1. If the final is outside F(R), use the already-proved two agreement tails
   for that same arbitrary R and adaptive final. Their pointwise moment is
   not multiplied by a family cardinality.
2. Otherwise, if no good representative exists, any representative Q chosen
   from the prechallenge family is bad. If the actual prior is zero, the
   actual compact first-discrepancy polynomial for that Q evaluates to zero.
   The proof derives this equality from the executed weights and scalar;
   it does not supply it as a correspondence premise.
3. For one fixed Q, bad image contributes at most two tau roots plus six
   first-repair alpha roots. In the complementary image-valid wrong-row
   case, the shifted cubic row error contributes at most three kappa roots
   plus six first-repair roots. These are disjoint cases fixed before kappa;
   their uniform ceiling is `3/(k-1)+6/k`, not their sum.
4. Only those early exceptional events are union-bounded over at most 99
   quotients. The one actual shifted degree-q rho batch and the three later
   degree-six relation repairs are attached **once** to the entire supported
   event, across both represented and unrepresented finals.

Responses know earlier challenges: response0 may depend on kappa and tau,
the final on alpha as well, and each later compact response on the preceding
queries/challenges. No target is frozen before its actual fixing point.
The family can depend on gamma/OOD; this proof uses it only before the later
kappa/tau/alpha challenges and does not use it for a gamma root count.
