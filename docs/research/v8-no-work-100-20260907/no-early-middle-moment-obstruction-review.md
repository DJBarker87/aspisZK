## Exact existing interfaces

`SelectedHigherYNoEarlySupport.no_early_support_cover` proves that when the
literal option `EarlyC1Projection.earlyC1 e.c1` is `none`, the fixed
original-code Good set has at most 63 gammas. Outside it, every image-valid
Q has `fibreCount <= 252847`. This does not lower that cap to 200807.

`SelectedHigherYBranch.higherPrefix` retains the SAME actual Q in its
literal-family, `not badAnchor`, adaptive final-fold identity and retained
higher-prime root. Its repaired row errors are zero already; one cannot
apply a wrong-row theorem to this event. `Qualified`, used by the existing
regular support tail, retains image/support/factor root but not the ordinary
row equations. This is an actual potential strengthening point, not an
already proved improved bound.

`RelationCompatibleMoment.compatibleMoment` is definitionally

    if p.prior final = 0 then matchingRatio D q final received else 0.

`suffix_bound` and `causal_supported_bound` preserve this gate and charge
the shared `q/|G| + 18/|A|` once. They give no smaller numerical bound on
the zero-prior case. `SelectedRegularQueryBridge.regular_matching_moment_bound`
then drops the gate. `RegularQueryMoment.fixed_fold_moment` gives

    beta(m) + (1-beta(m))*min(1,3/|A|),
    beta(m) = choose(m,q)/choose(262144,q).

The selected low-support layer-cake instance restricts m to at most 200807.
Its fixed-factor tail extends to larger m using
`SelectedRegularTailSum.tail_sum_bound`, but it retains the same budget:

    (4*m-1026) * tailSum(4*m-2) <= 1048576 * 239599331.

`SelectedRegularLowProbability.low_probability_bound` cannot simply be
applied to the middle band: its `LowPrefix` is the disjoint complement of
the high-support event, not a generic arbitrary support interval. Extending
its construction requires a new event/table instantiation, and the existing
numerical tail is insufficient even after that source plumbing.

## Why retaining the prior gate alone cannot help

The draft `compatibleMoment_eq_matching_of_prior_zero` exposes the exact
equality under prior zero. The additional draft proves an actual compact
grammar instance, rather than assuming a response correspondence:
`zeroSent` sets the six sent fields c0,c1,c2,c3,c5,c6 to zero. With incoming
claim zero, `nextClaim_source_horner` makes the reconstructed quartic and
carried claim zero at EVERY alpha. The zero final has dot product zero
against EVERY transported fold weight. Thus `zero_response_moment` equals
the full matching ratio, regardless of tau, ordinary weights or the
arbitrary received word. The response is constant before alpha, so this
does not exploit an illegal post-alpha first response.

More generally, a correct image/row candidate allows its honest convolution
to be sent before alpha. The fact that a candidate has imperfect received
support does not make its ordinary relation false. Consequently there is
no universal extra alpha/tau/kappa factor to be recovered from the current
moment gate on the remaining row-correct branch.

The normalized cubic moment bound itself is tight in a source-shaped
reduced model. Fix Q=0, a full-fibre set S, and three distinct alpha roots.
Set the error to zero on S. On every other fibre use the literal inverse
radix-4 evaluator of the SAME cubic h(alpha), in deployed slot order
`(x,y),(x,-y),(-x,-y),(-x,y)`. The normalized circle butterfly returns h on
every bad fibre. At its three roots all queries match; otherwise exactly
the fibres in S match. Prior zero therefore gives exactly

    beta(|S|) + (1-beta(|S|))*3/|A|.

The Rust fixture checks this mechanism, not only h's roots. It uses all seven
nondegenerate F31 circle fibres, natural8/final2 order, six full fibres,
q=3, all 31 alphas and all 210 ordered distinct schedules. It also checks
the six-field Horner/prior for every kappa/tau/alpha (29791 cases), using
shifted ordinary and image-weight formulas. The expected exact count is
3990 successful alpha/schedules out of 6510, namely 19/31, saturating
`4/7+(1-4/7)*3/31`. A small rank check on two full fibres rules out exact
polynomiality of the nonzero corrupted received word. These are specified
assertions that all passed in the retained optimized run.

This is an obstruction to improving the current generic moment theorem.
It is NOT a counterexample to a theorem that genuinely exploits the
selected interpolant kernel, actual higher-factor membership or payment
semantics. The fixture does not claim a full `MiddleWitness`, decoded
payment, authentication, or a gamma-level success probability. Small-model
ordinary covectors are explicitly not the selected ten-bit MLE covectors.

## Exact quantitative requirement

The already saved `higher-y-fixed-c1-screen.json` records 97.1879093395618
bits for the naive q22 layer cake through 252847, including Good63,
singular117049 and the shared repair. This audit did not rerun that screen.
A new small arithmetic read uses its stored integer numerators and only
22 multiplicative steps for each binomial coefficient:

    extended query numerator / choose(262144,22)
      = 117709496.15970881 gamma units;
    available query budget outside the pair-root event
      = 16660060.96875 gamma units approximately;
    required uniform improvement = 7.065370071604277.

The alpha correction is only three gamma units at this scale. Removing it
entirely would not produce the missing factor. Charging it a second time
or multiplying a separate gamma marginal into an alpha/query moment is
not justified.

One sufficient alternative is a cardinality bound on the actual
row-qualified middle UNION, rather than the larger per-factor tail. Keep
the existing low contribution, whose stored query numerator is
931472.6969096907 gamma units. Since beta(252847) is approximately
0.4518344681484129, an upper bound of 34810510 on that middle-union gamma
cardinality is sufficient for the outside-pair-root arithmetic. The
existing additive tail at m=200808 gives 313184030, so that coarse repair
needs a genuinely smaller count. All numbers here are arithmetic targets,
not a newly proved probability.

## Smallest sound next repairs

### 1. Same-source row-qualified gamma tail (no protocol change)

Fix the received C1/C2, OOD data, weights and claims before gamma. Choose
the already justified common regular row and its root polynomial Z. Define
the middle set using the original Gamma, NOT a renormalized complement:

    gamma in Gamma, gamma outside Good, Z(gamma) != 0,
    exists kappa,tau,alpha,Q,
      SelectedHigherYHighSupport.Witness e gamma kappa tau alpha Q,
      200808 <= fibreCount(e.raw gamma,Q) <= 252847.

A concrete sufficient new theorem is that this set has cardinality at most
34810510, or a correspondingly stronger weighted tail sufficient for the
displayed 16660060.96875-unit budget. Such a theorem must USE the zero
ordinary-row errors, the actual source covectors/claims, and their relation
to the same fixed received words. The old `Qualified` support bound alone
does not prove it. A generic assertion that linear constraints automatically
shrink high-support incidence is not enough: a 1024-dimensional message
space still has a large joint kernel of three row covectors and two OOD
evaluations. Singular roots and Good remain separately charged, and the
same suffix is repaired once after unioning the event.

This is a proof refinement for the unchanged source grammar, with no
inherent transcript/byte/verifier-CU change. It remains an open algebraic
theorem, not a supplied hypothesis to be relabelled as completed security.

### 2. A genuine fresh coherence holdout (a different causal obligation)

The checked `MiddleCoherentIncidence.coherent_middle_card_le_36` bounds
gammas for ONE fixed tuple with insufficient own support. After constructing
that tuple from 29 nodes, at most seven additional distinct coherent middle
nodes remain. A fresh draw from the full Gamma has at most 36/|Gamma| such
values; a proved uniform-unused draw after excluding the 29 training nodes
has at most 7/(|Gamma|-29). Neither value can be charged to the training
nodes themselves.

The missing source implication is that the tested successful continuation
has `original(Q_gamma)=batch gamma p` for the FROZEN tuple, or that failure
of this equality has a separately proved charge. Eight more middle nodes
do not supply it: the exact 37-node interpolation counterexample in
`middle-coherent-incidence-review.md` makes all eight fail coherence.

Using a holdout only inside an offline extractor does not change proof bytes
or verifier CU, but it also does not yet bound actual acceptance: the
rewinding/selection and acceptance-to-coherence argument still has to be
proved. Requiring a new committed tuple or checked challenge in the verifier
would be a protocol change with new transcript/byte/CU accounting, not a
free proof repair. No such change is authorized or implemented here.
